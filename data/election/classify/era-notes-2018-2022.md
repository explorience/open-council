# Era notes — 2018-2022 term (batches 40-65)

Gate round 9 fixer notes: two items in the round's brief required no
correction, but are recorded here (and in the PR body) so the reasoning
isn't lost.

## 1. Career-layer axis-label adjacency (3cacd5dd213d)

`3cacd5dd213d` (2019-06-18 Corporate Services Committee item 2.8, Wharncliffe
Road South expropriation, 5-1) renders "expanded general-purpose road
capacity" under `issue: transit`, `axis: road-capacity`. That's correct as
classified — road-capacity is one of the two axes under the `transit` issue
cluster (see `direction-rules.ts`'s 2026-08-31 transit/road-capacity split:
transit-service for bus/BRT content, road-capacity for general-purpose lane
widenings, both filed under the `transit` issue umbrella) — but on a career
page that shows a councillor's votes across MULTIPLE terms/eras, the label
"expanded general-purpose road capacity" needs to stay visually adjacent to
its axis context so a reader doesn't misread a road-widening vote as a
transit-SERVICE vote. This is a **design requirement for the career-layer
renderer**, not a data defect in this entry — noted for whoever builds or
reviews that renderer, not fixed here.

## 2. Percent-levy vote carries no direction (272eaf831e6f)

`272eaf831e6f` (2019-05-21 Council item 8.4.5, 10-4) is the era's most
quotable levy vote — a 2020-2023 multi-year planning target of "approximately
2.7%" average annual tax levy increase — and it is currently `axis: null`,
`polarity: null` (issue=budget, no direction). This is **rule-correct, not a
gap**: the levy-size axis's expansive/restrictive rules in `direction-rules.ts`
are keyed to a **dollar** figure (a `$` levy-effect line), and this clause's
own operative text prints a **percentage** target, not a dollar amount, so
no direction is mechanically derivable from it under the axis's current
rule. Extending the levy-size axis (or adding a new axis) to also derive a
direction from a percent-target clause is a real, deliberate rule change —
worth doing, since it's the term's single most quotable levy vote — but it's
a decision for the humans reviewing this gate, not something a fixer should
silently invent a new derivation rule for. Left `unclear` as the rules
currently stand.
