Recommendation: Plus Jakarta Sans

  • Modern and approachable without being too playful
  • Excellent legibility at all sizes
  • Works well for both headings and body text
  • Open source and free to use (Google Fonts)
  • Good x-height makes it readable on mobile
  • Pairs well with the existing color scheme
