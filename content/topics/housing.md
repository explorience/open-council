Housing policy covers affordable housing development, homelessness response through the Whole of Community System Response (WCSR), shelter operations, and regulations for additional residential units (ARUs).

## Key Areas

- **Affordable Housing**: Social housing, rent-geared-to-income, LMCH
- **Homelessness**: Shelters, hubs, encampment response, WCSR
- **Housing Supply**: ARUs, secondary suites, infill development
- **Housing Affordability**: Rent supplements, supportive housing
