Social services includes mental health and addiction support, seniors programs, childcare, accessibility improvements, and poverty reduction.

## Key Areas

- **Mental Health**: Crisis services, CMHA partnerships
- **Addiction**: Harm reduction, consumption sites, safe supply
- **Seniors**: Age-friendly initiatives, programs, long-term care
- **Accessibility**: AODA compliance, barrier-free design
- **Childcare**: Licensed childcare, early learning
