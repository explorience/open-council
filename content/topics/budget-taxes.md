Budget and tax policy includes the annual operating budget, multi-year capital budget, property tax rates, and funding priorities.

## Key Areas

- **Operating Budget**: Annual spending on city services
- **Capital Budget**: Infrastructure, facilities, major projects
- **Property Taxes**: Tax levy, mill rate, assessment
- **Funding**: Grants, reserves, debt financing
