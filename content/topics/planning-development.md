Planning and development covers zoning decisions, development applications, heritage preservation, and the Official Plan.

## Key Areas

- **Zoning**: Rezoning applications, variances, Official Plan amendments
- **Development**: Site plans, subdivisions, building permits
- **Heritage**: Heritage designations, conservation, historic districts
- **Appeals**: Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) decisions
