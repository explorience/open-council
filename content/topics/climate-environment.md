Environmental policy includes the Climate Emergency Action Plan (CEAP), urban forestry, conservation areas, and stormwater management.

## Key Areas

- **Climate Action**: CEAP, emissions reduction, net zero targets
- **Urban Forest**: Tree planting, canopy protection, street trees
- **Conservation**: UTRCA, wetlands, environmentally significant areas
- **Sustainability**: Green buildings, renewable energy, waste diversion
