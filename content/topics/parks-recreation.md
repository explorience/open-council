Parks and recreation covers parkland, trails like the Thames Valley Parkway, community centers, pools, arenas, and sports facilities.

## Key Areas

- **Parks**: New parkland, upgrades, maintenance
- **Trails**: Thames Valley Parkway (TVP), multi-use paths
- **Recreation**: Community centers, programs, accessibility
- **Facilities**: Pools, arenas, sports fields, playgrounds
