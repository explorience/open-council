Infrastructure includes roads, bridges, water and sewer systems, stormwater management, and waste collection.

## Key Areas

- **Roads**: Paving, repairs, construction, arterials
- **Water/Sewer**: Water mains, wastewater, stormwater
- **Bridges**: Repairs, replacements, pedestrian bridges
- **Waste**: Garbage collection, recycling, landfill (W12A)
