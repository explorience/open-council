Transportation policy in London includes public transit (London Transit Commission), cycling infrastructure, parking regulations, traffic management, and pedestrian safety initiatives.

## Key Areas

- **Public Transit**: Bus routes, BRT rapid transit, transit hubs
- **Cycling**: Bike lanes, multi-use pathways, cycling network
- **Parking**: Overnight parking, permits, enforcement
- **Traffic**: Signal timing, speed limits, traffic calming
- **Pedestrians**: Sidewalks, crosswalks, accessibility
