The City Council of London, Ontario serves as the primary governing body for Canada's 11th largest city, making key decisions that affect over 400,000 residents. As the main legislative authority, the Council is responsible for setting municipal policy, approving the annual budget, passing bylaws, and overseeing city operations across all departments. The Council typically meets twice monthly during regular session periods, with additional special meetings called as needed to address urgent matters or specific issues requiring immediate attention.

The Council addresses a broad spectrum of municipal issues including urban planning and development applications, transportation and infrastructure projects, public safety initiatives, environmental policies, and community services. They regularly review staff reports on topics such as zoning amendments, road construction projects, affordable housing strategies, waste management, parks and recreation programming, and economic development initiatives. The Council also handles citizen concerns, approves contracts with external organizations, and makes decisions on land acquisitions or disposals. Their work directly impacts daily life in London through decisions on property taxes, service delivery, and long-term city planning that shapes the community's growth and development.
