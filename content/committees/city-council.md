The City Council of London, Ontario serves as the primary governing body for Canada's 11th largest city, representing approximately 430,000 residents. As the main decision-making authority, the Council is responsible for setting municipal policy, approving the city budget, enacting bylaws, and overseeing the delivery of essential city services. The Council typically addresses a wide range of municipal matters including urban planning and development applications, infrastructure projects, public transit initiatives, housing policies, environmental sustainability measures, and community safety programs.

With 169 meetings held to date, including both regular and special sessions, the Council demonstrates an active approach to municipal governance. The frequent meeting schedule, which includes special sessions for urgent or complex matters, reflects the Council's role in responding to evolving community needs and addressing time-sensitive issues. The Council's work encompasses everything from routine administrative approvals to major strategic decisions that shape London's growth, economic development, and quality of life for its residents.
