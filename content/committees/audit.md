The Audit Committee of London City Council serves as an independent oversight body responsible for ensuring accountability, transparency, and sound financial management within the municipal government. This committee typically reviews the city's financial statements, internal controls, and compliance with policies and regulations. It works closely with both internal and external auditors to examine the effectiveness of the city's risk management processes, internal audit functions, and financial reporting systems.

The committee plays a crucial role in city governance by providing objective scrutiny of municipal operations and acting as a check and balance on city administration. It addresses issues such as reviewing audit findings and management responses, monitoring the implementation of audit recommendations, overseeing the annual financial audit process, and ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations. The Audit Committee also examines operational audits that assess the efficiency and effectiveness of city programs and services, helping to identify areas for improvement and cost savings that benefit taxpayers.
