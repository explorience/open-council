The Planning and Environment Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that plays a crucial role in shaping the city's development and environmental policies. This committee typically handles matters related to urban planning, zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives that affect London, Ontario. As one of the key decision-making bodies in municipal governance, it reviews and makes recommendations on land use planning, subdivision applications, heritage conservation, and various environmental protection measures.

The committee serves as an intermediary between city staff, developers, residents, and Council, carefully examining proposed developments and their potential impacts on the community. With 287 meetings held to date, this committee demonstrates the ongoing nature of urban development and environmental stewardship in a growing city like London. The committee's work directly influences how the city grows, where new developments are approved, and how environmental considerations are integrated into municipal planning decisions, making it one of the most impactful committees in terms of residents' daily lives and the city's long-term sustainability.
