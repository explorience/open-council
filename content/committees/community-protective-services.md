The Community and Protective Services Committee is one of the standing committees of London City Council that plays a crucial role in municipal governance by overseeing services that directly impact residents' daily lives and safety. This committee typically handles matters related to public health, social services, emergency services, bylaw enforcement, housing initiatives, and community safety programs. As an active committee that has held 196 meetings to date, it serves as a forum for reviewing policies, budgets, and service delivery in areas such as fire and paramedic services, police board oversight, public health programs, affordable housing strategies, and various community support services.

The committee's role in city governance involves making recommendations to City Council on matters within its jurisdiction, reviewing staff reports, hearing public input on relevant issues, and ensuring that London's community and protective services meet the needs of its residents. The committee addresses a wide range of issues including emergency preparedness, homelessness strategies, mental health and addiction services, animal control, licensing and regulation of businesses, and various social programs designed to support vulnerable populations. Through regular meetings and special sessions when needed, the committee ensures ongoing oversight and strategic planning for essential municipal services that contribute to community well-being and public safety.
