The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees various public safety and protective services within the city. This committee typically handles matters related to police services, fire and emergency services, bylaw enforcement, emergency management, and other community safety initiatives. As part of London's municipal governance structure, the committee serves as a key decision-making body that reviews policies, budgets, and operational matters for departments responsible for protecting residents and maintaining public safety.

The committee addresses a wide range of issues including police budget approvals, fire service deployment strategies, emergency preparedness plans, bylaw updates and enforcement policies, and community safety programs. Members examine reports from service chiefs, consider public input on safety concerns, and make recommendations to City Council on matters affecting public protection. The committee plays a crucial role in ensuring that London's protective services are adequately funded, properly managed, and responsive to evolving community needs and safety challenges.
