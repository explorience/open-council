The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees municipal services related to public safety, emergency response, and community protection. This committee typically handles matters concerning fire services, paramedic services, emergency management, bylaw enforcement, and other protective services that ensure the safety and well-being of London residents. The committee serves as a governance body that reviews policies, budgets, and strategic initiatives related to these critical municipal services before making recommendations to City Council.

As part of the city's committee structure, the Community Protective Services Committee plays a vital role in municipal governance by providing focused oversight and policy development for emergency and protective services. The committee addresses issues such as fire department operations and equipment needs, paramedic service delivery and response times, emergency preparedness planning, bylaw enforcement priorities, and public safety initiatives. Members review staff reports, hear delegations from the public, and make decisions on matters within their mandate, ensuring these essential services meet community needs and operate effectively.
