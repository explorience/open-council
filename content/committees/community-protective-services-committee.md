The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees the city's public safety and emergency services operations. This committee plays a crucial role in city governance by providing oversight and policy direction for essential protective services that safeguard London's residents and visitors. The committee serves as the primary liaison between City Council and the various departments responsible for community protection and emergency response.

The committee typically addresses issues related to fire prevention and suppression services, emergency medical services, emergency management and disaster preparedness, bylaw enforcement, and other community safety initiatives. Their work includes reviewing departmental budgets, evaluating service delivery standards, addressing public safety concerns raised by residents, and ensuring that London's protective services meet current and future community needs. The committee also considers policy recommendations, operational updates, and strategic planning matters that impact the effectiveness and efficiency of the city's protective services infrastructure.
