The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees the city's public safety and protective services. This committee plays a crucial role in municipal governance by providing oversight and policy direction for services that protect residents and maintain community safety. The committee serves as a bridge between City Council and the various departments responsible for emergency response, public safety, and community protection services.

The committee typically addresses issues related to fire services, emergency management, paramedic services, police oversight (where applicable to municipal jurisdiction), and other protective services that fall under the city's mandate. This includes reviewing budgets, service delivery standards, operational policies, and strategic planning for these critical municipal services. The committee also considers matters such as emergency preparedness, public safety initiatives, community risk assessment, and the allocation of resources to ensure effective protective services across London's communities.
