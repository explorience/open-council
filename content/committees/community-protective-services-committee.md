The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees the city's public safety and emergency services functions. This committee typically handles matters related to police services, fire protection, emergency management, bylaw enforcement, and other community safety initiatives. As part of London's municipal governance structure, the committee reviews policies, budgets, and operational matters for services that protect residents and maintain public safety throughout the city.

The committee serves as a crucial link between city administration and council, examining reports and making recommendations on issues such as police budget allocations, fire department operations, emergency preparedness plans, and community safety programs. Their work directly impacts how London responds to emergencies, enforces municipal bylaws, and maintains public order. The committee's deliberations help ensure that protective services are adequately funded, properly managed, and aligned with community needs and council priorities.
