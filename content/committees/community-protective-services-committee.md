The Community Protective Services Committee is a standing committee of London City Council that oversees the city's public safety and emergency services operations. This committee typically handles matters related to police services, fire department operations, emergency management planning, and other community safety initiatives. As part of London's municipal governance structure, the committee serves as a liaison between city council and various protective service agencies, reviewing budgets, policies, and strategic plans for these essential services.

The committee addresses a wide range of issues affecting community safety and well-being, including fire prevention programs, emergency response protocols, community policing initiatives, and disaster preparedness planning. Members review reports from service chiefs, consider policy recommendations, and make decisions on resource allocation for protective services. The committee also examines performance metrics, responds to community safety concerns, and ensures that London's protective services meet provincial standards and community needs.
