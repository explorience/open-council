The Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee (formerly the Corporate Services Committee) is a standing committee of London City Council that plays a crucial role in municipal governance by overseeing two key areas of city operations. The committee is responsible for corporate services functions, which typically include human resources, information technology, legal services, finance and budget matters, procurement, risk management, and general administrative operations that keep the city running efficiently. Additionally, the committee oversees infrastructure-related matters such as public works, roads and transportation, water and wastewater systems, facilities management, and capital project planning and implementation.

As evidenced by its 280 meetings to date, this committee handles a substantial workload and serves as a vital link between city administration and elected officials. The committee reviews staff reports, makes recommendations to City Council on policy matters, approves expenditures within their mandate, and ensures that both the city's physical infrastructure and corporate operations meet the needs of London's residents. The recent transition in naming from "Corporate Services Committee" to "Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee" reflects the expanded scope of responsibilities and the critical importance of infrastructure planning and maintenance in municipal governance.
