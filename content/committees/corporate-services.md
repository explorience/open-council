The Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee (formerly the Corporate Services Committee) serves as a key standing committee of London City Council, responsible for overseeing critical municipal operations and strategic planning. This committee typically handles infrastructure development and maintenance, including roads, utilities, public facilities, and capital projects that support the city's growth and functionality. On the corporate services side, the committee addresses administrative functions such as human resources, information technology, financial management, procurement policies, and organizational governance matters.

As an integral part of London's municipal governance structure, this committee reviews staff reports, makes recommendations to City Council on policy matters, and provides oversight on major municipal investments and service delivery improvements. The committee's dual focus on both physical infrastructure and corporate administration makes it central to ensuring the city operates efficiently while planning for future needs. With 280 meetings held to date, this committee demonstrates the ongoing nature of municipal governance and the continuous need to address evolving infrastructure requirements and administrative challenges facing the growing city of London.
