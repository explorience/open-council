The Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee (formerly the Corporate Services Committee) serves as a key standing committee of London City Council, responsible for overseeing the city's operational infrastructure and internal administrative functions. This committee typically handles matters related to municipal infrastructure planning and maintenance, including roads, utilities, public facilities, and transportation systems. On the corporate services side, the committee addresses human resources policies, information technology systems, financial management practices, procurement processes, and other administrative functions that support the city's day-to-day operations.

As an integral part of London's municipal governance structure, this committee reviews staff reports, makes recommendations to City Council on policy matters, and provides oversight of major infrastructure projects and corporate initiatives. The committee serves as a forum for public input on infrastructure and administrative issues, ensuring transparency in municipal decision-making. With 280 meetings held to date, including both regular and special sessions, the committee demonstrates the ongoing nature of municipal infrastructure and administrative oversight, addressing everything from budget allocations and service delivery improvements to long-term strategic planning for the city's growth and development.
