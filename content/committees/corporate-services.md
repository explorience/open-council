The committee oversees corporate services functions, which typically include human resources, information technology, financial management, legal services, clerk services, and general administrative operations that support the overall functioning of city government.

The committee also handles infrastructure-related matters, which encompasses the planning, development, maintenance, and financing of the city's physical assets including roads, bridges, water and wastewater systems, public buildings, and other essential municipal infrastructure. By combining these two areas of responsibility, the committee serves as a vital link between the administrative backbone of city operations and the physical infrastructure that supports London's growth and daily operations. The committee reviews staff reports, makes recommendations to City Council on policy matters, oversees major capital projects, and ensures that both corporate services and infrastructure investments align with the city's strategic priorities and budget constraints.
