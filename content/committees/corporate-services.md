The Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee (formerly the Corporate Services Committee) is a standing committee of London City Council that plays a crucial role in municipal governance by overseeing two key areas of city operations. The committee handles infrastructure matters including transportation systems, roads, water and wastewater services, public transit, and capital projects that form the backbone of the city's physical infrastructure. Additionally, it manages corporate services such as financial planning and budgeting, human resources policies, information technology systems, procurement processes, risk management, and other administrative functions that support the municipality's day-to-day operations.

As evidenced by its 280 meetings to date, this committee serves as an important deliberative body that reviews staff reports, makes recommendations to City Council, and provides oversight on significant municipal expenditures and policy decisions. The committee's dual mandate reflects the interconnected nature of infrastructure development and corporate governance in municipal administration. Recent meetings suggest an active agenda addressing both immediate operational needs and long-term strategic planning for London's growth and development. The committee's work directly impacts citizens through decisions about service delivery, infrastructure investments, tax implications, and the overall efficiency of municipal government operations.
