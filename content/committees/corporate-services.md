The Infrastructure and Corporate Services Committee (formerly the Corporate Services Committee) is a standing committee of London City Council that plays a crucial role in municipal governance by overseeing the city's operational backbone and administrative functions. This committee typically handles matters related to municipal infrastructure including roads, utilities, public works, and transportation systems, as well as corporate services such as human resources, information technology, procurement, financial management, and administrative policies. The committee serves as a key decision-making body that reviews and makes recommendations to City Council on capital projects, service delivery improvements, and strategic initiatives that support the city's daily operations.

With 280 meetings held to date, this committee demonstrates the ongoing and substantial nature of municipal administration required to serve London's residents and businesses. The committee's work encompasses both long-term strategic planning for infrastructure development and maintenance, as well as immediate operational concerns such as budget approvals, contract awards, policy updates, and service level agreements. Their decisions directly impact citizens' daily lives through the quality of roads, availability of municipal services, efficiency of city operations, and the responsible stewardship of taxpayer funds across various departments and initiatives.
