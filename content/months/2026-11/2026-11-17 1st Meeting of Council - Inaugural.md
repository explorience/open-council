## 10.1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Deputy Mayors and Budget Chair

[2026-11-17 Submission - Deputy Mayors and Budget Chair-J. Morgan](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=127458>)

> Motion made by S. Lewis

> Seconded by Mayor J. Morgan

That the following actions BE TAKEN with respect to the Appointment of Deputy Mayor and Budget Chair Policy and the Remuneration for Elected Officials and Appointed Citizen Members Policy:

a) the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to bring forward a by-law to the November 17th meeting of Municipal Council to amend By-law No. CPOL.-417-268 being the “Appointment of Deputy Mayor and Budget Chair Policy” to provide for the provision of two Deputy Mayors; one serving a four-year term, the other a two-year term; and

b) the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to bring forward a by-law to the November 17th meeting of Municipal Council to amend By-law No. CPOL.-70-302 being the “Remuneration for Elected Officials and Appointed Citizen Members Policy” to delete section 4.4 and replace it with the following new section 4.4:

“Additional compensation for Deputy Mayor and Budget Chair positions shall be equal to 5% of the annual base remuneration established for a Councillor. The additional compensation provided under this section shall be adjusted concurrently with any annual adjustment to Councillor remuneration under this Policy.”;

it being noted that the City Clerk acknowledges, with thanks, the communication received from Hon. Robert J. Flack, Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing.

> **Motion Passed**

****
