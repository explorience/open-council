## 5.2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(ADDED) Community Housing Financial Challenges

That the following actions be taken with respect to Community Housing Financial Challenges:

a)    the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to report back to a future meeting of the Community and Protective Services Committee with a summary of the number of units currently vacant within the community housing system in the Service Manager area and collect additional data from community housing providers and provide an analysis of the key factors contributing to the vacancies;

b)    the following actions BE TAKEN with respect to this matter because the committee is concerned that the current conditions constitute a threat to the health and safety to the residents of Tolpuddle and request that the matter of treating the deficiencies to be an emergency which would warrant immediate action:

i)         the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to immediately advance the funds and approvals necessary for the 2025-2026 COCHI Projects (security system, fire monitoring system) for Tolpuddle Housing Co-operative as outlined in Appendix G of the Feb 17, 2026 Community and Protective Services Committee Item 2.5 Affordable and Community Housing Update as approved projects and without any conditions regarding a lien payment; and,

ii)         the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to report back to the Community and Protective Services Committee meeting on April 13, 2026 on the lien situation regarding Tolpuddle Housing Co-operative including the amount, any concerns regarding the amount, supports to be offered and a timeline;

c)    the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to provide the following details based on the 2024 data used in Appendix B of the staff report, dated February 17, 2026, entitled “Affordable and Community Housing Update”, to the Community and Protective Services Committee:

 

i)         a list of the 43% of Housing Providers who “do not generate and retain enough cash to pay for short-term debts” and the number of units in each (RGI, etc.);

ii)         a list of the 25% of Housing Providers whose “future survival” is at “significant risk”; and,

iii)         a brief summary of each of the above with the estimated timeline for insolvency, the funding source and amount available for these issues and the manner in which any Service Manager intervention will be reported to Council;

it being noted that the following items, with respect to this matter, were received:

-    a communication from Councillor C. Rahman, dated March 5, 2026;
