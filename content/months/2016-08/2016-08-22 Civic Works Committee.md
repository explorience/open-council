  $2,171,500.00, including contingency, excluding H.S.T.; it being noted that
  the requirements of this provincial funding program are unique, in that only
  conservation authorities can apply, requiring 14.3.a) of the Procurement of
  Goods and Services Policy:

  London Dykes Phases 3 - 9 Design;
ii)          London
  Earth Dykes Preliminary Engineering Feasibility;

  Dam Transformer Station and Standby Generator Replacement;
iv)        West
  London Dyke Phase 3 Construction;

  Dyke Environmental Assessment;

  Dyke Environmental Assessment;

  Repairs Broughdale & Clarence-Nelson;

  Motor Control Centre Replacement;
