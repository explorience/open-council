## 2.1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Single Source Procurement SS18-34 - Occupational Health Services Provider

[2018-12-11 Staff Report - Single Source Procurement](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=56055>)

> Moved by J. Morgan

> Seconded by S. Hillier

That, on the recommendation of the Managing Director, Corporate Services and Chief Human Resources Officer, the following actions be taken with respect to the single source acquisition of an Occupational Health Services provider for The Corporation of the City of London under section 14.4 (d) of the Procurement of Goods and Services Policy:



a)            the Civic Administration BE AUTHORIZED to negotiate terms acceptable to Civic Administration to continue to acquire Occupational Health Services through its current provider, Workplace Medical Corporation, on the basis that the current fees for services (less than $60,000/year) will remain unchanged for a contract term of four (4) years with an option for one (1) additional year;



b)            the Civic Administration BE AUTHORIZED to undertake all administrative acts that are necessary in connection with this project;



c)            the approvals given herein BE CONDITIONAL upon the Corporation entering into a formal contract for the work to be done relating to this project; and,



d)            the Mayor and the City Clerk BE AUTHORIZED to execute any contract or other documents, if required, to give effect to these recommendations.

> **Motion Passed**
