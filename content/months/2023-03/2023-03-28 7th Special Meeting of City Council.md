### 8.1.2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[(2.1)](</2023-03/2023-03-21 The 6th Meeting of the Community and Protective Services Committee#212nd-report-of-the-accessibility-community-advisory-committee>) Next Generation 9-1-1 Transition Funding Support - Transfer Payment Agreements

> Motion made by E. Peloza

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services, the following actions be taken with respect to the staff report, dated March 21, 2023, related to the Next Generation 9-1-1 Transition Funding Support Transfer Payment Agreements:

a)     the proposed by-law, as appended to the above-noted staff report, BE INTRODUCED at a special meeting of Municipal Council to be held on March 28, 2023, to:

i)    approve the Next Generation 9-1-1 Transition Funding Support Transfer Payment Agreement between His Majesty the King in right of Ontario, as represented by the Solicitor General, and The Corporation of the City of London (London Police Service-Communications Section), substantially in the form appended to the above-noted by-law;

ii)    delegate authority to the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services to execute the above-noted Agreement; 

iii)    delegate authority to the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services to approve and execute any further amending agreements to the above-noted Transfer Payment Agreement; 

iv)    authorize the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services, or written delegate, to execute any financial reports required under the above-noted Agreement; and,

v)    delegate authority to the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services to approve and execute a future agreement between The Corporation of the City of London and the London Police Services Board assigning the terms of Schedule ‘A’ of Appendix ‘A’ to the London Police Services Board; and,

b)    the proposed by-law, as appended to the above-noted staff report, BE INTRODUCED at a special meeting of Municipal Council to be held on March 28, 2023, to;

i)    approve the Next Generation 9-1-1 Transition Funding Support Transfer Payment Agreement, between His Majesty the King in right of Ontario, as represented by the Solicitor General, and The Corporation of the City of London (London Fire Department-Communications Division), substantially in the form appended to the above-noted by-law;

ii)    delegate authority to the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services to execute the above-noted Agreement;  

iii)    delegate authority to the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services, or written designate, to undertake all the administrative, financial and reporting acts that are necessary in connection with the above-noted Agreement; and,

iv)    authorize the Deputy City Manager, Neighbourhood and Community-Wide Services, or written delegate, to execute any financial reports required under the above-noted Agreement.

> **Motion Passed**
