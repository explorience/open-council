## 2.4&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;RFT 19-90 Plumbing Services at Various City of London Facilities - Irregular Result

## 2.5&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Pre-Authorized Tax Payment Plan By-law and Collection of Property Taxes By-law
