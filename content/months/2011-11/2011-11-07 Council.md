  deferred consideration of the Thames Valley Corridor Plan (Business Case, p.

That the Services Review Committee (SRC)
  deferred consideration and review of the Snow Removal business case (p. 71)
  to a future meeting of the SRC, with a recommendation to be forwarded to the
  Municipal Council in conjunction with the 2012 Budget;  it being noted that
  the previous additional snow removal funding was not considered to have made
  a significant impact, and that the business case would result in a $375,000
  cut to the overall Budget; it being further noted that the Civic
