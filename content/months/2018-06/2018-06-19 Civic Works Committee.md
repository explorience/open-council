[2018-06-19 SR - Parking Regulation Surveys](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=46832>)

> Moved by T. Park

> Seconded by H. Usher

i.        25% (or greater) of the property owners support a review of the parking regulations on their street; and
