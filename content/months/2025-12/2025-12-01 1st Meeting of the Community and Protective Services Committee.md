## 2.3&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Approval of Contribution Agreement with Canadian Mental Health Association Thames Valley Addiction and Mental Health Services - 644-646 Huron Street

[2025-12-01 SR Approval of Contribution Agreement with CMHA TVAMHC - 644-646 Huron Street - Part 1](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=120775>)

[2025-12-01 SR Approval of Contribution Agreement with CMHA TVAMHC - 644-646 Huron Street - Part 2](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=120776>)

> Moved by D. Ferreira

> Seconded by S. Trosow

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth, the following actions be taken with respect to the staff report dated December 1, 2025 related to the Approval of Contribution Agreement with Canadian Mental Health Association Thames Valley Addiction and Mental Health Services, 644-646 Huron Street:

a)    the Canadian Mental Health Association Thames Valley Addiction & Mental Health Services (“CMHA Thames Valley”)’s Highly Supportive Housing project at 644-646 Huron Street BE APPROVED for Ontario Priorities Housing Initiative Year 7 (“OPHI”) funding of $1,374,335.00 and the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to recommend this project for funding to the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing; and,

b)    the proposed by-law, as appended to the above-noted staff report, BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting on December 16, 2025, to:

i)    APPROVE the Contribution Agreement between The Corporation of the City of London and CMHA Thames Valley (the “Contribution Agreement”), as appended to the above-noted by-law;

ii)    AUTHORIZE the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth, or their written designate, to approve amendments to the Contribution Agreement;

iii)    AUTHORIZE the Mayor and the City Clerk to execute the Contribution Agreement; and,

iv)    AUTHORIZE the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth, or their written designate, to approve and execute further amending agreements to the Contribution Agreement.

> **Motion Passed**

## 2.4&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;122 Baseline Road Update and Property Management Agreement

[2025-12-01 SR 122 Base Line Road Update and Property Management Agmt - Part 1](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=120770>)

[2025-12-01 SR 122 Base Line Road Update and Property Management Agmt - Part 2](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=120771>)

[2025-12-01 SR 122 Base Line Road Update and Property Management Agmt - Part 3](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=120772>)

> Moved by D. Ferreira

> Seconded by S. Trosow

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth, the following actions be taken with respect to the staff report dated December 1, 2025 related to 122 Base Line Road Update and Property Management Agreement:

a)    the above-noted staff report BE RECEIVED for information in response to Recommendation #1 of the London Housing Development Lessons Learned audit;

b)    the attached proposed by-law, as appended to the above-noted staff report, BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting to be held on December 16, 2025 to:

i)    APPROVE the Property Management Agreement between The Corporation of the City of London and London & Middlesex Community Housing Inc. as appended to the above-noted by-law as Schedule 1;

ii)    AUTHORIZE the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth to approve amendments to the above-noted Agreement; 

iii)    AUTHORIZE the Mayor and the City Clerk to execute the above-noted Agreement; and,

iv)    AUTHORIZE the Deputy City Manager, Housing and Community Growth, or their written designate, to approve and execute amending agreements to the above-noted Agreement that do not require additional funding;

c)    the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to work with London and Middlesex Community Housing on an expedited tenant transition plan that supports the long-term financial sustainability of the property;

d)    the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to report back annually on progress; and,

e)    the Source of Financing, as appended to the above-noted staff report BE APPROVED to provide $500,000 in capital for necessary immediate enhancements to the building, and an additional $200,000 for unit rehabilitation, as outlined in Section 4.1 and 4.2.
