# 11.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;4th Report of the
  Animal Welfare Advisory Committee

Recommendation: That the following actions
  be taken with respect to the 4th Report of the Animal Welfare Advisory
  Committee, from its meeting held on March 7, 2013:
 
a)           
  clause
  1 BE REFERRED to the Civic Administration for consideration, with a
  report back to the Community and Protective Services Committee (CPSC) on the
  feasibility of the recommendation; it being noted that the CPSC received a
  verbal presentation from S. Rans and A. Papmehl of the AWAC with respect to
  this matter; 
 
clause 1, as referred, reads as follows:
 
“That the Civic Administration BE REQUESTED
  to adopt practices for pest, rodent and wildlife control that are consistent
  with the Municipal Council’s June 26, 2012 resolution “to support and
  implement a progressive and humane response program related to human-wildlife
  conflicts in the municipality”; it being noted that the Animal Welfare
  Advisory Committee (AWAC) reviewed and received the attached communication
  dated March 7, 2013, from A. Papmehl, with respect to this matter.”
 
b)         clauses
  2 to 13, BE RECEIVED.

> |-|
