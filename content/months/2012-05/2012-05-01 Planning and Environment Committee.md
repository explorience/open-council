  Borer Strategy and Issues Related to Tobin Court

Recommendation:  That, the following
  actions be taken with respect to the Westmount Hills Residents Association’s
  concerns relating to the emerald ash borer:
 
a)           
  the Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to continue with the
  implementation of the Emerald Ash Borer Strategy that was adopted by the
  Municipal Council at its meeting held on October 3, 2011;
 
b)           
  the
  Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to utilize a temporary signage
  program to advise neighbourhoods, in advance, that Emerald Ash Borer removal
  will be occurring in their neighbourhood and directing residents to the
  Emerald Ash Borer web site;
 
c)           
  the
  Civic Administration BE DIRECTED to determine the legal and financial
  impact of property owners injecting infested trees on their properties, at
  their expense and report back to a future meeting of the Planning and
  Environment Committee; and,
 
d)           
  the
  attached presentation from the Westmount Hills Residents Association BE
  REFERRED to the Civic Administration for consideration and to report back
  to a future meeting of the Planning and Environment Committee.
