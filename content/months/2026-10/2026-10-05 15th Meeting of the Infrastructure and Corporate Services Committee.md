## 2.2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2026 Mid-Year Operating Budget Monitoring Report

[2026-10-05 Staff Report - (2.2) 2026 Mid-Year Operating Monitoring](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=126770>)

> Moved by S. Franke

> Seconded by E. Peloza

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Finance Supports, the following actions be taken with respect to the 2026 Mid-Year Operating Budget Monitoring Report:



a)    the 2026 Operating Budget Mid-Year Monitoring Report for the Property Tax Supported Budget, Water Budget, and Wastewater and Treatment Budget BE RECEIVED for information. An overview of the corporate projections are outlined below, noting actual results could fluctuate based on factors beyond the control of the Civic Administration:



i)    Property Tax Supported Budget projected surplus of $13.6 million;

ii)    Water Rate Supported Budget projected deficit of $0.8 million; 

iii)    Wastewater and Treatment Rate Supported Budget projected surplus of $1.4 million;

 

it being noted that the Property Tax, and Wastewater & Treatment Budget surplus, if realized, will be allocated in accordance with the Council-approved Surplus/Deficit Policy;

it being further noted that the Water deficit, if realized, will be funded through a draw from the Water Budget Contingency Reserve in accordance with the Council-approved Surplus/Deficit Policy.

> **Motion Passed**

b)    the status updates of active 2023 life-to-date capital projects (2023 and prior) having no future budget requests, as appended to the staff report dated October 5, 2026 as Appendix “B”, BE RECEIVED for information;



c)    the following actions be taken with respect to the completed capital projects identified in Appendix “C”;



i) the capital projects included in Appendix “C”, with net surplus funding of $4.5 million, BE CLOSED;

ii) the following actions be taken with respect to the funding associated with i), above;



Rate Supported

i) pay-as-you-go funding of $197 thousand BE TRANSFERRED to capital receipts;

ii) authorized but unissued debt financing of $199 thousand BE RELEASED from the capital budget; 

iii) uncommitted reserve fund drawdowns of $1.3 million BE RELEASED back into the reserve funds which originally funded the projects;

Non-Rate Supported

iv) uncommitted reserve fund drawdowns of $2.7 million BE RELEASED back into the reserve funds which originally funded the projects;

v) other net non-rate supported funding adjustments of $66 thousand BE APPROVED in order to facilitate project closings;



d)     the budget adjustments noted in section 2.6 of this report BE APPROVED to adjust the capital projects:Z



i) SH3000P – Queen’s Place Infrastructure Project be funded by the Building Canada Strong Fund – Community Stream (Formerly Canada Community-Building Fund) and return the original source of financing to the SH3000 – Roadmap to 3,000 Affordable Units parent project;

ii) TS1670 – Intersection – Sarnia / Philip Aziz – Western Road be reduced by $8.7 million, with $1.1 million of tax-supported debenture financing released from the capital budget and $7.6 million of non-rate supported reserve fund (development charges) financing released back to City Services Roads Reserve Fund; and

iii) TS1411 – Kilally – Webster Street to Clarke Road be reduced by $3.8 million, with $433 thousand of tax-supported debenture financing and $3.4 million in non-rate supported debenture (development charges) financing being released from the capital budget.

## 2.10&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Pre-Authorized Tax Payment Plan By-law and Collection of Property Taxes By-law

[2026-10-05 Staff Report - (2.10) Pre-Authorized Tax Payment Plan](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=126763>)

> Moved by S. Franke

> Seconded by E. Peloza

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Finance Supports, the following actions be taken with respect to property taxation for 2027:

a)    the by-law as appended to the staff report dated October 5, 2026 as Appendix 'A' BE INTRODUCED at the Council meeting to be held on October 14, 2026, to amend By-law A.-5505-497 being “a by-law to authorize the implementation of a pre-authorized payment plan” so that the calculation of pre-authorized payments is based on the previous year’s taxes increased by the average increase in total property tax rates in the residential class of the previous year; and

b)    the by-law as appended to the staff report as Appendix 'B' BE INTRODUCED at the Council meeting to be held on October 14, 2026, to amend By-law A-8426-294 entitled “Property Tax Collection by-law” so that the calculation of the interim tax levy will be set at a percentage of 41.21% of the previous year’s taxes.

> **Motion Passed**
