## 3.1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Not to be heard before 1:05 PM - Public Participation Meeting - 2027 Water and Wastewater Rates

[2026-10-06 Staff Report - (3.1) 2027 Water and Wastewater Rates](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=126778>)

> Moved by E. Peloza

> Seconded by A. Hopkins

That, on the recommendation of the Deputy City Manager, Environment & Infrastructure and the Deputy City Manager, Finance Supports, the following actions be taken with respect to the 2027 Water and Wastewater rates and charges:



a)    the proposed by-law, as appended to the staff report dated October 6, 2026 as Appendix “A”, to amend By-law WM-28 being “A by-law for regulation of wastewater and stormwater drainage systems in the City of London” BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting to be held on October 14, 2026, to effect rates and charges increases of 5.1 percent effective January 1, 2027; and



b)    the proposed by-law, as appended to the staff report as Appendix “B”, to amend By-law W-8 being “A by-law to provide for the Regulation of Water Supply in the City of London” BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting to be held on October 14, 2026, to effect rates and charges increases of 4.8 percent effective January 1, 2027;



it being noted that the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee received a communication from L. Claro with respect to this matter;

it being further noted that no individuals spoke at the public participation meeting associated with this matter.

## 3.2&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Not to be heard before 1:05 PM - Public Participation Meeting - 2027 Amendments to Consolidated Fees and Charges By-law

[2026-10-06 Staff Report - (3.2) 2027 Amendments to Consolidated Fees and Charges](<https://pub-london.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=126779>)

That, on the recommendation of the City Clerk, with the concurrence of the Deputy City Manager, Finance Supports the proposed by-law, as appended to the staff report dated October 6, 2026, as Appendix “B” BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting to be held on October 14, 2026, to repeal By-law A-62 and replacing it with a new 2027 Consolidated Fees and Charges By-law that lists various fees and charges for services or activities provided by the City of London, including a revision to the proposed Schedule 1 appended to the staff report dated October 6, 2026, as Appendix “B”, to maintain the existing 2026 rates for City of London delivered registered and drop-in programs for individuals and families, thereby freezing those rates at the 2026 levels for 2027;

it being noted the revenue impact of maintaining the existing 2026 rates for City of London delivered registered and drop-in programs for individuals and families, thereby freezing those rates at the 2026 levels for 2027 BE REFERRED to the 2027 Budget Update process;

it being further noted that the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee received a communication dated October 6, 2026 from Mayor J. Morgan and Councillor S. Franke with respect to this matter;

it being acknowledged that no individuals spoke at the public participation meeting associated with this matter.
