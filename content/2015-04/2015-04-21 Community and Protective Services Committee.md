# 8.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ban on Shark Fin Products

That the following actions be taken with
  respect to a ban on shark fin products:
 
a)         on
  the recommendation of the Managing Director, Development and Compliance
  Services and Chief Building Official and the City Clerk, the proposed by-law,
  as appended to the staff report dated April 21, 2015, to repeal By-law No.
  PH-17, being “A by-law to prohibit the possession, purchase, sale, trade,
  distribution, consumption, or preparation for consumption of Shark Fins in
  the City of London” BE INTRODUCED at the Municipal Council meeting to be held
  on April 28, 2015; it being noted that the Municipal Council remains
  concerned with the treatment of sharks for the removal of fins; and, 
 
b)         the
  appropriate Federal and Provincial Ministers BE REQUESTED to bring forward
  appropriate legislation to ban the importation, and prohibit the possession,
  purchase, sale, trade, distribution, consumption or preparation for
  consumption of shark fins.  (2015-C01)
