London City Council's 2022 operations demonstrate the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees throughout the year. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing work of managing urban development, zoning applications, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) served as the primary decision-making body where major policies, budgets, and bylaws received final approval.

The committee structure reveals the breadth of municipal responsibilities that London City Council manages. The Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings addressed critical infrastructure decisions including road construction, water systems, and public works projects, while the Corporate Services Committee's 13 meetings likely focused on budget deliberations, human resources, and administrative policies. The Community and Protective Services Committee handled public safety, social services, and community programs through 12 meetings. The Audit Committee's 4 meetings, though fewer in number, provided essential oversight of financial accountability and governance practices, ensuring transparency in the city's operations and spending.
