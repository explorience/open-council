London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings underscore the ongoing work of managing urban development, zoning applications, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) served as the formal decision-making body where major policies, budgets, and bylaws received final approval.

The distribution of committee meetings reflects typical municipal priorities: infrastructure and public works (Civic Works Committee), financial oversight and administrative matters (Corporate Services Committee), and community services including public safety, recreation, and social programs (Community and Protective Services Committee). These committees collectively handle the essential functions of city governance, from approving annual budgets and capital projects to reviewing development applications, setting tax rates, managing public transit, overseeing police and fire services, and addressing housing and social policy issues. The Audit Committee's four meetings, while fewer in number, represent crucial financial oversight ensuring accountability in public spending.
