London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, conducting 94 meetings across seven key committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the ongoing focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings demonstrate the significant attention given to urban development, zoning applications, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) served as the primary decision-making forum where major policies, budgets, and strategic directions were formally adopted.

The committee structure reflects the breadth of municipal responsibilities, with Civic Works addressing infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works, while Corporate Services managed financial planning, human resources, and administrative functions. The Community and Protective Services Committee oversaw social programs, public safety, and community development initiatives. Throughout these meetings, council members made critical decisions affecting residents' daily lives, including annual budget approvals, development permits, infrastructure investments, service delivery improvements, and policy responses to emerging community challenges. The relatively fewer Audit Committee meetings (4) suggests a focused approach to financial oversight and accountability review.
