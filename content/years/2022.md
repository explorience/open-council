London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the extensive scope and frequency of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the ongoing need to address long-term city planning and policy development. Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the continuous decisions around zoning changes, development applications, environmental regulations, and urban growth management that are essential to a growing city like London.

The distribution of meetings across committees reflects typical municipal priorities: Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings focused on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; Corporate Services Committee's 13 meetings handled budget oversight, financial planning, and administrative matters; while Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings addressed public safety, social services, and community programming. Regular City Council meetings (16) provided the forum for final decision-making on budgets, bylaws, and major policy initiatives, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency in city operations.
