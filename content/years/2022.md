London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven committees addressing the full spectrum of urban administration. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 17 meetings dedicated to zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives. City Council itself convened 16 times to make final decisions on major items forwarded from committees.

The committee structure reflects the core responsibilities of municipal government: the Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings addressed infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public transit; Corporate Services' 13 meetings handled budget deliberations, human resources, and administrative policies; while Community and Protective Services' 12 meetings covered social programs, public safety, and community development. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential oversight of financial accountability. This meeting frequency illustrates how councils balance strategic visioning with day-to-day operational decisions, managing everything from multi-million dollar infrastructure investments to neighborhood zoning changes that affect residents' daily lives.
