London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, holding 94 meetings across seven committees to address the city's diverse municipal needs. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on high-level municipal direction and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to handle zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. The full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues, with specialized committees like Civic Works (14 meetings) managing infrastructure projects, roads, and utilities, and Corporate Services (13 meetings) overseeing financial matters including budget deliberations, procurement, and administrative policies.

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the essential functions of municipal government: approving annual budgets and financial plans, making zoning decisions that shape community development, authorizing infrastructure investments in roads and public facilities, and establishing policies for community services like recreation programs, public safety, and social services. The Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings addressed public health, housing, and emergency services, while the Audit Committee's four meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. This meeting structure reflects the complex, multi-layered nature of municipal governance, where elected officials must balance competing priorities while ensuring effective service delivery to residents.
