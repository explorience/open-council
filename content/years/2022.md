London City Council's 2022 meeting schedule reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 total meetings distributed across seven key committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, indicating significant focus on long-term planning and policy development, while Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing importance of land use decisions, zoning amendments, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council meetings (16) served as the primary decision-making body where major policies, budgets, and strategic directions were formally approved.

The distribution of committee meetings illustrates the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from infrastructure planning and public works handled by the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings) to financial oversight and administrative matters addressed by Corporate Services (13 meetings). The Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings covered essential services like public health, housing, and emergency services, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided financial accountability oversight. This structure enables councillors to make informed decisions on critical municipal matters including annual budgets, development approvals, infrastructure investments, service delivery standards, and regulatory bylaws that directly impact residents' daily lives.
