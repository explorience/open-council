London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a growing municipality. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. City Council itself convened 16 times for formal decision-making on major issues including budget approval, bylaw enactment, and strategic direction setting.

The operational committees - Civic Works, Corporate Services, and Community and Protective Services - collectively held 39 meetings to manage the city's day-to-day functions. These committees would have addressed critical municipal responsibilities such as infrastructure maintenance and capital projects, financial management and human resources, public safety services, and community programming. The Audit Committee's four meetings, while fewer in number, served the essential oversight function of ensuring financial accountability and transparency. This meeting structure reflects the complex, multi-layered decision-making process required to govern a modern city, from neighborhood-level zoning changes to multi-million dollar infrastructure investments.
