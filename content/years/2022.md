London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the substantial workload required to manage a major municipal government. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term city planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. City Council itself convened 16 times for formal decision-making on major issues including budget approval, bylaw adoption, and strategic direction setting.

The distribution of committee meetings reflects the core responsibilities of municipal government: infrastructure planning and maintenance through the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings), corporate governance and financial oversight via Corporate Services (13 meetings), and community services delivery through the Community and Protective Services Committee (12 meetings). These committees typically handle decisions ranging from annual budget allocations and tax rate setting to zoning amendments and development permits, road construction and maintenance projects, public transit planning, social services programming, and regulatory enforcement. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability mechanisms to ensure responsible stewardship of public resources.
