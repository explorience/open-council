London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, conducting 94 meetings across seven committees and full council sessions. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings demonstrate the ongoing attention to development applications, zoning changes, and environmental initiatives that shape the city's growth. Full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues brought forward from the various committees.

Throughout these meetings, council members addressed the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities that affect residents' daily lives. This included deliberating on annual budgets and financial planning, reviewing and approving zoning amendments and development proposals, overseeing infrastructure projects like road construction and utility upgrades, and making decisions about community services such as recreation programs, public safety initiatives, and social services. The Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings likely focused on infrastructure and public works projects, while the Corporate Services Committee's 13 meetings would have covered administrative matters, human resources, and financial oversight. The relatively lighter meeting schedule for the Audit Committee (4 meetings) reflects its more specialized role in financial accountability and governance review.
