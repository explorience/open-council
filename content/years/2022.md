London City Council's 2022 work schedule demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees throughout the year. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, handling the city's long-term vision and major policy directions, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. City Council itself convened 16 times for formal decision-making on major issues, budgets, and bylaw approvals.

The remaining committees focused on essential municipal services: Civic Works (14 meetings) managed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; Corporate Services (13 meetings) oversaw financial management, human resources, and administrative functions; Community and Protective Services (12 meetings) addressed public safety, social services, and community programs; while the Audit Committee met quarterly (4 meetings) to ensure financial accountability and risk management. This meeting structure reflects the typical work of municipal councils, which make critical decisions about annual budgets, land use planning, infrastructure investments, service delivery, and policy development that directly impact residents' daily lives.
