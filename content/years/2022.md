London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing work of managing urban development, zoning applications, and environmental initiatives. The 16 regular City Council meetings served as the primary forum for final decision-making on major issues affecting residents.

Throughout these meetings, council members addressed the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities that define local government. This included deliberating on annual budgets and financial planning, reviewing zoning changes and development applications, overseeing infrastructure projects like road maintenance and public transit, and managing community services such as recreation programs and public safety initiatives. The Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings focused on infrastructure and public works, while the Corporate Services Committee's 13 meetings dealt with administrative and financial matters. The Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings addressed social services, housing, and public safety, with the Audit Committee's 4 meetings providing financial oversight and accountability.
