London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the intensive, year-round nature of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven committees to address the diverse needs of the community. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and major policy initiatives, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing work of managing urban development, zoning applications, and environmental stewardship. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) served as the central decision-making body where recommendations from committees were formally debated and adopted.

The distribution of committee meetings reflects typical municipal priorities: infrastructure planning and maintenance through the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings), financial oversight and administrative decisions via Corporate Services (13 meetings), and community safety and social services coordination through Community and Protective Services (12 meetings). These committees would have addressed core municipal responsibilities including annual budget deliberations, development approvals, road construction and repairs, bylaw enforcement, emergency services, parks and recreation programs, and regulatory compliance. The Audit Committee's four meetings, while fewer in number, represent crucial oversight of financial accountability and transparency in city operations.
