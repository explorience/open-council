London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, conducting 94 meetings across seven key committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing urban development pressures facing the city. Full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues brought forward from the various committees.

Throughout these meetings, council members addressed the fundamental responsibilities of municipal governance: approving annual budgets and financial plans, making zoning decisions that shape neighborhood development, overseeing infrastructure projects like road repairs and transit improvements, and ensuring community services meet residents' needs. The distribution of meetings across committees reflects typical municipal priorities, with significant time devoted to planning applications, public works projects, corporate administration, and community safety initiatives. The Audit Committee's four meetings, while fewer in number, represent crucial oversight ensuring taxpayer dollars are managed responsibly and transparently.
