London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees throughout the year. City Council itself convened 16 times to make final decisions on matters ranging from annual budgets and tax rates to major infrastructure investments and community services.

The distribution of meetings across committees reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government. The Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings likely addressed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works, while the Corporate Services Committee's 13 sessions would have covered financial management, human resources, and administrative policies. The Community and Protective Services Committee held 12 meetings to oversee social programs, public safety, and community development initiatives. With only 4 Audit Committee meetings, this suggests a focused approach to financial oversight and accountability. Together, these committees managed the full spectrum of municipal decisions that directly impact residents' daily lives, from transit and housing to parks and emergency services.
