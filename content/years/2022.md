London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. City Council itself convened 16 times for formal decision-making on major issues including budget approvals, bylaw enactment, and strategic direction setting.

The committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, with Civic Works (14 meetings) handling infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works, while Corporate Services (13 meetings) managed financial oversight, human resources, and administrative matters. The Community and Protective Services Committee (12 meetings) addressed social services, public safety, and community programs, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability. This meeting frequency illustrates how councils balance immediate operational needs with long-term strategic planning, making decisions that affect everything from neighborhood development and public transit to emergency services and cultural programming.
