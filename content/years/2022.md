London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the significant time devoted to long-term planning, budget deliberations, and policy development that guides the city's direction. Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing work of managing urban development through zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives, while the 16 full City Council meetings served as the formal venue for final votes on major bylaws, budget approval, and strategic decisions.

The remaining committees each played crucial roles in London's governance: Civic Works addressed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works with 14 meetings; Corporate Services managed financial oversight, human resources, and administrative matters through 13 meetings; and Community and Protective Services coordinated social programs, public safety, and community services with 12 meetings. The Audit Committee's 4 meetings, while fewer in number, provided essential financial oversight and accountability. This meeting structure reflects the typical municipal council work of balancing immediate operational needs with long-term strategic planning, managing public resources, and responding to community needs across diverse portfolios.
