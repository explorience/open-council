London City Council's 2022 work exemplifies the comprehensive governance required to manage a major Canadian city, with 94 committee and council meetings addressing the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term city planning, budget oversight, and policy development that guides London's future growth and development. Meanwhile, the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings handled critical land-use decisions, including zoning amendments, development applications, and environmental initiatives that shape how the city grows and adapts to changing needs.

The remaining committees each played vital roles in city operations: City Council's 16 formal meetings ratified major decisions and budgets; Civic Works' 14 meetings managed infrastructure projects like roads, water systems, and public works; Corporate Services oversaw financial management, human resources, and technology through 13 meetings; and Community and Protective Services addressed social programs, housing, emergency services, and public safety through 12 meetings. The Audit Committee's 4 meetings, while fewer in number, provided crucial financial oversight and accountability. Together, these meetings represent hundreds of decisions on everything from multi-million dollar infrastructure investments and annual budget allocations to individual development permits and community program funding.
