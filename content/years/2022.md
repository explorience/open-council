London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022 with 94 meetings across seven committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a major Canadian city. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development proposals, and environmental initiatives. Full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues, supported by specialized committees handling civic infrastructure (Civic Works), municipal finances and human resources (Corporate Services), and community safety and social services (Community and Protective Services).

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the core responsibilities of municipal government: approving annual budgets and financial plans, making zoning and land-use decisions that shape the city's growth, overseeing infrastructure projects like road construction and utility upgrades, and establishing policies for public services including transit, housing, and emergency services. The Audit Committee's four meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency. This meeting structure reflects the complex, multi-layered nature of municipal governance, where technical committee work feeds into broader policy decisions that ultimately shape residents' daily lives and the city's long-term development.
