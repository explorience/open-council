London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the significant time devoted to setting long-term municipal direction, budget planning, and policy development. Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing demands of urban development, including zoning decisions, development applications, environmental initiatives, and growth management that shapes London's future.

The distribution of meetings across committees reveals the breadth of municipal responsibilities. City Council's 16 formal sessions focused on final decision-making and bylaw approvals, while Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings addressed critical infrastructure needs like road maintenance, water systems, and public transit. Corporate Services Committee's 13 meetings handled internal operations including human resources, IT systems, and financial management, while Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings dealt with social services, public safety, recreation programs, and community development. The Audit Committee's 4 meetings, though fewer in number, provided essential oversight of financial accountability and risk management.
