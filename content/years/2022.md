London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, conducting 94 meetings across seven key committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to handle zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) served as the primary decision-making body for major municipal matters.

The committee structure reflects the broad scope of municipal governance, with specialized groups handling infrastructure projects and public works (Civic Works), financial management and human resources (Corporate Services), and community safety and social services (Community and Protective Services). These committees typically address critical municipal functions including annual budget deliberations, infrastructure investments, zoning bylaw amendments, development proposals, service delivery improvements, and policy reforms. The Audit Committee, meeting quarterly, ensured financial oversight and accountability in municipal operations.
