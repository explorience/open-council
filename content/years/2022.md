London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven specialized committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term city planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. The full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues, supported by the operational work of the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings) handling infrastructure projects like road maintenance and public works.

The remaining committees rounded out the governance structure with Corporate Services managing budgets, human resources, and administrative matters (13 meetings), Community and Protective Services overseeing social programs, public safety, and bylaw enforcement (12 meetings), and the Audit Committee ensuring financial oversight and accountability (4 meetings). This meeting distribution reflects typical municipal priorities: strategic planning, development management, infrastructure maintenance, financial stewardship, and community services delivery. Together, these committees addressed the fundamental responsibilities of city government, from approving annual budgets and capital projects to reviewing development applications and establishing policies that shape London's growth and quality of life.
