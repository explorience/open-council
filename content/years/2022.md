London City Council's 2022 schedule reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. Regular City Council sessions convened 16 times to make final decisions on major issues and coordinate the work of all committees.

The remaining committees tackled essential municipal functions: Civic Works addressed infrastructure projects like road construction, water systems, and public transit; Corporate Services managed budgets, human resources, and administrative policies; and Community and Protective Services oversaw public safety, social programs, and community development. The Audit Committee's four meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability. This meeting structure demonstrates how modern city councils balance strategic planning with day-to-day operations, making decisions that impact everything from annual budgets and tax rates to individual development permits and emergency services.
