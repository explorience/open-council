London City Council's 2022 work demonstrates the breadth and complexity of municipal governance, with 94 meetings held across seven key committees throughout the year. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the significant time needed to establish the city's long-term vision, develop policy frameworks, and coordinate major initiatives. Meanwhile, the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings highlight the ongoing demand for development approvals, zoning decisions, and environmental assessments as London continues to grow and evolve.

The distribution of meetings across committees reflects typical municipal priorities: infrastructure decisions through Civic Works (14 meetings), budget oversight and administrative matters via Corporate Services (13 meetings), and community safety and social services coordination through Community and Protective Services (12 meetings). These committees collectively address the core functions of city government - from approving annual budgets and capital projects to reviewing development applications, updating bylaws, managing public services like transit and utilities, and ensuring proper financial oversight. The relatively fewer Audit Committee meetings (4) is standard, as this body focuses on specific financial reviews and compliance matters that require less frequent but more intensive examination.
