London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, holding 94 meetings across seven committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, reflecting the importance of long-term planning and policy development, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings underscore the ongoing challenges of urban development and environmental stewardship. Regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) provided the forum for final decision-making on major issues, supported by specialized committees handling civic infrastructure, corporate administration, and community services.

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the fundamental responsibilities of municipal government: approving annual budgets that allocate taxpayer funds across departments and services, making zoning decisions that shape neighborhood development, and overseeing critical infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public transit. The committee structure allowed for detailed examination of complex issues, from reviewing development applications and environmental assessments to setting policies for police services, social programs, and economic development initiatives that impact residents' daily lives.
