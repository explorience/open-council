London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2022, conducting 94 meetings across seven committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 18 meetings, focusing on long-term municipal planning and major policy initiatives, while the Planning and Environment Committee held 17 meetings to handle critical decisions around zoning changes, development applications, and environmental regulations. Full City Council convened 16 times to ratify major decisions and set overall municipal direction.

The remaining committees addressed specialized areas of municipal responsibility: Civic Works tackled infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works with 14 meetings; Corporate Services managed budgetary matters, human resources, and administrative policies through 13 meetings; and Community and Protective Services oversaw social programs, public safety, and recreational facilities with 12 meetings. The Audit Committee's four meetings focused on financial oversight and accountability measures. This structure reflects the typical scope of municipal governance, where councils make decisions on annual budgets, approve development projects, maintain public infrastructure, deliver community services, and ensure regulatory compliance across all aspects of city operations.
