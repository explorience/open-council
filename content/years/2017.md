London City Council's 2017 work demonstrates the complex and multifaceted nature of municipal governance, with 104 committee meetings addressing the full spectrum of city operations. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 25 meetings, handling essential administrative functions like human resources, finance, and technology systems. Close behind, the Planning and Environment Committee met 20 times to make critical decisions about zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies that shape the city's growth and sustainability. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 19 meetings focused on long-term planning and policy development that guides the city's future direction.

Infrastructure and service delivery dominated much of the remaining committee work, with Civic Works meeting 16 times to address roads, utilities, waste management, and public works projects, while Community and Protective Services held 15 meetings covering police, fire, social services, and recreational programs. The Audit Committee's 7 meetings provided crucial oversight of financial controls and performance management. Notably, formal Budget Committee and City Council meetings were minimal, suggesting most detailed work occurred at the committee level before proceeding to final approval through other governance processes.
