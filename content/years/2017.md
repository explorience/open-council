London City Council's 2017 operations demonstrate the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 104 committee meetings addressing the full spectrum of urban administration. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 25 meetings, handling essential business operations, human resources, and financial oversight. Planning and Environment Committee's 20 meetings focused on development applications, zoning changes, and environmental policies that shape the city's growth, while the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 19 meetings concentrated on long-term vision setting and policy development.

The remaining committees addressed critical municipal functions: Civic Works oversaw infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works through 16 meetings; Community and Protective Services managed social programs, public safety, and community services in 15 meetings; and the Audit Committee ensured financial accountability through 7 meetings. Notably, only single meetings were recorded for the Budget Committee and full City Council, likely representing major annual budget approval and formal ratification sessions where decisions from the various committees were consolidated and officially adopted.
