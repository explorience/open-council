London City Council's 2017 meeting schedule reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 104 meetings held across eight specialized committees. The Corporate Services Committee was the most active with 25 meetings, handling essential administrative functions like human resources, information technology, and financial management. The Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 20 meetings, addressing critical urban development issues including zoning applications, environmental assessments, and long-term city planning initiatives that shape London's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees demonstrate the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 19 meetings focusing on long-term vision and policy development, to the Civic Works Committee's 16 meetings overseeing infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee's 15 meetings covered public safety, social services, and community programs. Notably, formal Budget Committee and City Council meetings were limited to one each, suggesting that budget deliberations and final decision-making were likely integrated into other committee structures throughout the year.
