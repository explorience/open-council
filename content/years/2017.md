London City Council's 2017 work demonstrates the extensive and varied nature of municipal governance, with 104 committee meetings addressing the full spectrum of city operations. Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 20 meetings, making critical decisions about zoning applications, development approvals, environmental policies, and urban planning initiatives that shape the city's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees each played vital roles in city governance: Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (19 meetings) focused on long-term planning and policy development; Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) addressed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; and Community and Protective Services Committee (15 meetings) oversaw public safety, social services, and community programs.
