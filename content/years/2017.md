London City Council's 2017 work demonstrates the extensive and diverse nature of municipal governance, with 104 committee meetings addressing the full spectrum of city operations. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 25 meetings, handling essential administrative functions like human resources, information technology, legal affairs, and financial management. Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 20 meetings, making critical decisions on zoning applications, development approvals, environmental policies, and urban planning initiatives that shape the city's growth and character.

The remaining committees each played vital roles in city governance: Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (19 meetings) focused on long-term vision and major policy directions; Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) addressed infrastructure needs including roads, utilities, and public works; and Community and Protective Services Committee (15 meetings) oversaw public safety, recreation, housing, and social services. The Audit Committee's 7 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. Notably, only one formal Budget Committee meeting and one full City Council meeting were held, suggesting these bodies convened as needed for specific major decisions while most detailed work occurred at the committee level.
