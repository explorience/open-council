London City Council's 2017 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 104 committee meetings addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee led with 25 meetings, handling critical administrative functions including financial management, human resources, and technology infrastructure. Meanwhile, the Planning and Environment Committee's 20 meetings focused on zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental policies that shape the city's growth and sustainability. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 19 times to address long-term planning initiatives and policy development that guide the city's future direction.

The remaining committees tackled equally essential municipal responsibilities: Civic Works addressed infrastructure maintenance, road construction, and public works projects through 16 meetings, while Community and Protective Services held 15 meetings covering public safety, social services, and community programs. The Audit Committee's 7 meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency in city operations. Notably, both the Budget Committee and City Council held only one meeting each, suggesting these were likely focused sessions for final budget approval and formal council decisions, with most of the detailed work occurring in the specialized committees before reaching full council for ratification.
