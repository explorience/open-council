London City Council's 2017 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 104 meetings across eight specialized committees addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 25 meetings, handling critical administrative functions like human resources, information technology, legal matters, and financial oversight. Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 20 meetings, reviewing development applications, zoning changes, environmental policies, and urban planning initiatives that shape the city's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees each played vital roles in municipal operations: Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (19 meetings) focused on long-term planning and policy development; Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) addressed infrastructure projects including roads, utilities, and public works; and Community and Protective Services Committee (15 meetings) oversaw social services, public health, emergency services, and community programs. The Audit Committee's 7 meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency. Notably, both Budget Committee and City Council held only one formal meeting each, suggesting that budgetary decisions and final council deliberations were concentrated into intensive sessions, while most detailed work occurred at the committee level before advancing to full council consideration.
