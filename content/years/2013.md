London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2013 with 122 committee meetings across eight distinct bodies, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal administration. The Corporate Services Committee led with 30 meetings, handling essential business operations, human resources, and administrative policies, while the Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 26 meetings focused on zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. The Community and Protective Services Committee held 22 meetings addressing public safety, emergency services, social programs, and community development, while the Civic Works Committee's 18 meetings concentrated on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, utilities, and public works.

The council's committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, from the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings setting long-term vision and policy direction, to the more specialized Audit Committee's 7 meetings ensuring financial accountability and transparency. The Budget Committee's 4 focused meetings were dedicated to the critical annual process of allocating taxpayer resources across city services and capital projects. Notably, City Council itself convened only twice, indicating that much of the detailed work occurred at the committee level before final decisions were brought to full council for ratification.
