London City Council's 2013 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 122 committee meetings addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee led with 30 meetings, handling critical administrative functions like human resources, finance, and technology systems that keep the city operating. The Planning and Environment Committee followed closely with 26 meetings, making crucial decisions about zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies that shape London's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees tackled equally important municipal responsibilities: Community and Protective Services (22 meetings) addressed public safety, social services, and community programs; Civic Works (18 meetings) managed infrastructure projects like roads, utilities, and public works; and Strategic Priorities and Policy (13 meetings) focused on long-term planning and policy development. Specialized committees like Audit (7 meetings) and Budget (4 meetings) ensured financial oversight and fiscal planning, while City Council itself met twice to ratify major decisions and provide overall governance direction.
