London City Council's 2016 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight different committees and council bodies. The Corporate Services Committee and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee were the most active, each holding 28 meetings, reflecting the significant time devoted to administrative oversight, financial management, and long-term planning initiatives. These committees typically handle decisions related to municipal budgets, staffing, technology systems, strategic planning, and policy development that guides the city's direction.

The remaining committees address the core services that directly impact residents' daily lives. The Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings involved zoning decisions, development approvals, environmental policies, and land use planning that shapes the city's growth. The Community and Protective Services Committee (16 meetings) focused on public safety, social services, recreation programs, and community health initiatives, while the Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) dealt with infrastructure projects, road maintenance, water systems, and public works. The Audit Committee's 10 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability, while formal City Council sessions and Budget Committee meetings provided final approval on major decisions and the annual municipal budget.
