London City Council's 2016 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight specialized committees and council sessions. The committee structure reflects the key areas of municipal responsibility: Corporate Services and Strategic Priorities & Policy Committees led the workload with 28 meetings each, handling budget planning, policy development, and administrative oversight. Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings focused on zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives, while Civic Works Committee's 16 meetings addressed infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works.

The Community and Protective Services Committee also held 16 meetings, dealing with social services, public safety, recreation programs, and bylaw enforcement. The Audit Committee's 10 meetings ensured financial accountability and oversight of city operations. With formal City Council sessions limited to just 3 meetings and a single Budget Committee meeting, much of the detailed work occurred at the committee level, where recommendations were developed before advancing to full council for final approval. This structure allows councillors to specialize in specific areas while ensuring comprehensive review of all municipal matters affecting London residents.
