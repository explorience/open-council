London City Council's 2016 activities demonstrate the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight specialized committees and council itself. The year's work was heavily focused on strategic planning and corporate services, with both the Corporate Services Committee and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee each holding 28 meetings—nearly one-quarter of all council activities. These committees likely addressed critical decisions around budget planning, policy development, human resources, and long-term municipal strategy that guides the city's direction.

The remaining committees tackled the day-to-day operations that directly impact residents' lives. The Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings would have addressed zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives, while the Civic Works Committee's 16 meetings focused on infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee also met 16 times to discuss social services, recreation programs, and public safety initiatives. Notably, the Budget Committee held only one formal meeting, suggesting that budgetary discussions were likely integrated into other committee work throughout the year, while the Audit Committee's 10 meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability.
