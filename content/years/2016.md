London City Council's 2016 operations demonstrate the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight specialized committees and full council sessions. The Corporate Services and Strategic Priorities and Policy committees were the most active, each holding 28 meetings, indicating significant focus on administrative operations, long-term planning, and policy development. These committees typically handle crucial decisions around budgets, strategic planning, human resources, and major policy initiatives that shape the city's direction.

The remaining committees addressed the core functions of municipal government through regular meetings: Planning and Environment (21 meetings) dealt with zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies; Community and Protective Services (16 meetings) oversaw public safety, social services, and community programs; while Civic Works (16 meetings) managed infrastructure projects, public works, and transportation systems. The Audit Committee's 10 meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability. Notably, full City Council met only three times, suggesting most detailed work occurred at the committee level before final ratification, while the single Budget Committee meeting likely represented the culmination of extensive budget preparations conducted through other committees.
