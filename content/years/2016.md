London City Council's 2016 operations demonstrate the extensive committee work that underpins municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight different committees and council sessions. The Corporate Services Committee and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee were the most active, each holding 28 meetings, indicating significant focus on administrative matters, long-term planning, and policy development. The Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings reflect the ongoing work of managing urban development, reviewing zoning applications, and addressing environmental concerns that are central to a growing city's needs.

The distribution of meetings across committees reveals the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from infrastructure decisions handled by the Civic Works Committee to community safety and social services addressed by the Community and Protective Services Committee. The Audit Committee's 10 meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability, while the single Budget Committee meeting likely focused on the intensive annual budget process. This structure allows council to make informed decisions on critical municipal matters including property tax rates, development approvals, road maintenance and construction projects, public transit, waste management, parks and recreation services, and emergency services funding.
