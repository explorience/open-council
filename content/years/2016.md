London City Council's 2016 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 meetings held across eight committees and council sessions. The heaviest workload fell on the Corporate Services Committee and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, each meeting 28 times throughout the year, indicating significant focus on administrative matters, long-term planning, and policy development. Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings reflect the ongoing demands of urban development, including zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental assessments that shape the city's growth.

The distribution of meetings across committees illustrates how municipal councils manage diverse responsibilities from infrastructure maintenance and public works through the Civic Works Committee, to community safety and social services via the Community and Protective Services Committee. With only three formal City Council meetings and one Budget Committee session, much of the detailed work occurs at the committee level, where councillors examine specific issues like road repairs, building permits, bylaw enforcement, parks and recreation services, and financial planning before bringing recommendations to the full council for final approval.
