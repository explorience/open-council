London City Council's 2016 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 123 committee and council meetings addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services and Strategic Priorities and Policy committees were the most active, each holding 28 meetings, reflecting the significant time spent on administrative oversight, long-term planning, and policy development. These committees typically handle crucial decisions around budgets, strategic planning, human resources, and major policy initiatives that shape the city's direction.

The remaining committees focused on specialized areas of municipal responsibility. The Planning and Environment Committee's 21 meetings dealt with zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies that guide the city's growth. Community and Protective Services, along with Civic Works (16 meetings each), addressed public safety, social services, infrastructure maintenance, roads, utilities, and public works projects. The Audit Committee's 10 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability, while the single Budget Committee meeting and three City Council meetings represent the formal decision-making processes where all committee recommendations are reviewed, debated, and approved by the full council.
