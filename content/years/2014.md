London City Council's 2014 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 109 committee meetings addressing the full spectrum of city operations. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 24 meetings, focusing on long-term planning, budget development, and major policy initiatives that shape the city's direction. Meanwhile, the Corporate Services Committee held 23 meetings to oversee financial management, human resources, and administrative functions that keep the city running efficiently.

The remaining committees tackled specialized areas crucial to residents' daily lives: Planning and Environment (22 meetings) handled zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental protection measures; Community and Protective Services (18 meetings) addressed public safety, social services, and community programming; and Civic Works (17 meetings) managed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works. The relatively few Audit Committee meetings (4) reflect its focused mandate of financial oversight, while the single City Council meeting likely represents a formal ratification session, as most detailed work occurs at the committee level before full council consideration.
