London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2021 with 99 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a major Canadian city. The meeting distribution reflects the council's primary focus areas, with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees leading with 17 meetings each, handling critical decisions around municipal finances, budgets, development applications, and zoning changes. City Council and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee followed closely with 16 meetings each, where councillors made final decisions on major policy directions, long-term planning initiatives, and strategic investments in the community's future.

The robust committee structure allowed councillors to thoroughly examine complex municipal issues before bringing them to full council for final approval. Civic Works Committee's 15 meetings addressed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works, while Community and Protective Services Committee's 14 meetings focused on social services, public safety, and community programs. The Audit Committee's four meetings, though fewer in number, provided crucial oversight of financial management and accountability. This comprehensive meeting schedule enabled council to address the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities, from day-to-day service delivery to major capital projects and policy reforms that shape London's development and quality of life.
