The Civic Works Committee focuses on infrastructure projects like road construction, water systems, and public transit, while the Community and Protective Services Committee oversees social services, public health, housing policy, and emergency services.

This meeting frequency illustrates the continuous decision-making required to govern a major Canadian city, with committees typically meeting monthly or bi-monthly to address both routine business and strategic initiatives. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 16 meetings suggest significant focus on long-term planning and policy development, while the full City Council's 16 meetings provided opportunities for final approvals, public input, and city-wide coordination.
