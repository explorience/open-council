London City Council's 2021 operations demonstrate the extensive governance work required to manage a major city, with 99 committee and council meetings held throughout the year. The meeting distribution reflects the core areas of municipal responsibility: Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees led with 17 meetings each, handling critical functions like financial management, human resources, development applications, and environmental policies. City Council itself convened 16 times for formal decision-making, while the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee also met 16 times to guide long-term planning and policy development.

The remaining committees addressed essential city services through regular meetings: Civic Works (15 meetings) focused on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; Community and Protective Services (14 meetings) oversaw social services, public health, and safety programs; while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. Throughout these meetings, council members made decisions on annual budgets worth hundreds of millions of dollars, approved zoning changes and development applications, allocated resources for infrastructure repairs and expansions, established bylaws and policies affecting residents' daily lives, and responded to emerging community needs and provincial requirements.
