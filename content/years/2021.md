London City Council's 2021 operations demonstrate the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 99 meetings held across seven committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The committee structure reflects the core areas of municipal responsibility: Corporate Services handled financial management, human resources, and administrative matters; Planning and Environment addressed zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies; Strategic Priorities and Policy focused on long-term city planning and policy development; while Civic Works managed infrastructure projects including roads, water systems, and public works.

The Community and Protective Services Committee dealt with social services, public health initiatives, and emergency services, while the Audit Committee provided financial oversight and accountability. Through these meetings, council members made critical decisions affecting residents' daily lives, from approving annual budgets and tax rates to reviewing development proposals that shape the city's growth. They also addressed infrastructure investments, public transit planning, emergency response protocols, and community programs, demonstrating how municipal government operates as the level of government closest to citizens' immediate concerns.
