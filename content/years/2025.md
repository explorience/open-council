London City Council's 2025 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 85 meetings held across seven specialized committees. The council's work spans critical areas of city management, from setting annual budgets and tax rates to making zoning decisions that shape community development. Through committees like Planning and Environment, council members review development applications, approve new subdivisions, and establish policies for environmental protection and urban growth. The Community and Protective Services Committee handles matters ranging from public health initiatives and social services to fire and police services, while Corporate Services manages the city's financial operations, human resources, and technology infrastructure.

The meeting distribution reveals the council's priorities and workload, with Corporate Services, Community and Protective Services, and full City Council each holding 16 meetings—reflecting the intensive work required for financial oversight, service delivery, and formal decision-making. Infrastructure investments, bylaw amendments, and strategic planning initiatives flow through committees like Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee before reaching full council for final approval. The Budget Committee's focused six-meeting schedule reflects the concentrated period of budget deliberations, while the Audit Committee's three meetings demonstrate ongoing financial accountability and oversight.
