London City Council's 2025 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance through 85 committee meetings across seven specialized bodies. The Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees led activity with 16 meetings each, handling critical areas like financial management, human resources, public safety, and community programs. City Council itself held 16 formal sessions to debate and vote on major policy decisions, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings addressed zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives that shape London's growth and sustainability.

The council's committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, from the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings focused on long-term vision and policy development, to the Budget Committee's 6 intensive sessions managing the city's financial planning and resource allocation. The Audit Committee's 3 meetings, while fewer in number, provided crucial oversight of financial accountability and governance practices. Throughout the year, councillors made decisions affecting residents' daily lives, including infrastructure investments, public transit improvements, housing policies, business licensing, emergency services, parks and recreation programs, and environmental protection measures.
