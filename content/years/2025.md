London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025 with 85 meetings across seven specialized committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The Corporate Services Committee and Community and Protective Services Committee led the way with 16 meetings each, handling critical areas like human resources, financial management, public safety, recreation services, and social programs. Full City Council also convened 16 times to make final decisions on major policies, bylaws, and strategic initiatives that shape London's future.

The meeting distribution reflects the varied nature of municipal governance, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 sessions addressing zoning applications, development proposals, and environmental policies, to the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings focusing on long-term city planning and major policy directions. The Budget Committee's 6 concentrated sessions were essential for reviewing departmental budgets, capital projects, and tax strategies, while the Audit Committee's 3 meetings provided crucial financial oversight. Together, these committees tackled decisions ranging from infrastructure investments and public transit planning to housing policies, business licensing, emergency services, and community development projects that directly impact residents' daily lives.
