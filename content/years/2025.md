London City Council's 2025 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 85 meetings held across seven specialized committees throughout the year. The bulk of council business was conducted through four main committees that each met approximately monthly: Corporate Services (16 meetings) handling financial management, human resources, and administrative matters; Community and Protective Services (16 meetings) overseeing public safety, social services, and community programs; regular City Council sessions (16 meetings) for final decision-making and public engagement; and Planning and Environment (15 meetings) managing urban development, zoning applications, and environmental initiatives.

The committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of modern municipal government, from approving annual budgets and multi-year capital infrastructure projects to reviewing individual development applications and zoning amendments. Council members made decisions affecting everything from road construction and public transit to affordable housing policies, emergency services funding, and environmental sustainability initiatives. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings focused on long-term planning and major policy directions, while the Budget Committee's 6 intensive sessions concentrated on the critical work of allocating taxpayer resources. The Audit Committee's 3 meetings provided essential oversight of financial accountability and risk management.
