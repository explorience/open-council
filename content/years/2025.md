London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, conducting 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The council's work was distributed among key areas, with Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees each holding 16 meetings to handle administrative functions, human resources, public safety, and community programs. Regular City Council meetings (16 sessions) served as the primary decision-making body where major policies, bylaws, and strategic initiatives were debated and voted upon.

The Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings focused on critical urban development decisions including zoning amendments, subdivision approvals, environmental assessments, and infrastructure planning that shape London's growth. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 13 times to guide long-term city planning, economic development strategies, and policy framework development. Financial oversight remained paramount with 6 dedicated Budget Committee meetings to scrutinize spending proposals, tax rates, and capital investments, while 3 Audit Committee sessions ensured fiscal accountability and governance standards. This comprehensive committee structure enabled council to effectively manage municipal responsibilities ranging from road maintenance and public transit to housing policy, business licensing, and emergency services coordination.
