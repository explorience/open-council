London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, holding 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The workload was fairly distributed, with core operational committees like Corporate Services, Community and Protective Services, and full City Council each meeting 16 times throughout the year. These committees tackled essential municipal responsibilities including budget allocation and financial oversight, zoning approvals and land use planning, infrastructure development and maintenance, public safety initiatives, and community services delivery.

The committee structure reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings focused on development applications and environmental policy, to the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 sessions dedicated to long-term city planning and policy development. The Budget Committee's 6 focused meetings concentrated on the critical annual budget process, while the Audit Committee's 3 meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability measures to ensure responsible stewardship of public resources.
