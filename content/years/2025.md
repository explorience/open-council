The council's decision-making process covered the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities, from approving annual budgets and capital expenditures through the Budget Committee's 6 meetings, to overseeing infrastructure projects, public safety initiatives, and community programs. Regular business included zoning amendments and development approvals, road construction and maintenance projects, public transit planning, social services delivery, and economic development initiatives. The Audit Committee's 3 meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability, while the various committees balanced immediate community needs with long-term strategic planning for London's continued growth and prosperity.
