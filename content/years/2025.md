London City Council's 2025 work demonstrates the comprehensive governance required to manage a growing city, with 87 meetings held across seven specialized committees. The majority of council business flows through four core committees that each met 16 times: Planning and Environment (handling development applications, zoning changes, and environmental initiatives), Corporate Services (overseeing technology, human resources, and administrative matters), Community and Protective Services (managing public safety, social services, and recreation), and full City Council sessions (where final decisions are ratified and major policy directions are set). The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, meeting 13 times, focuses on long-term planning and major initiatives that shape the city's future direction.

Beyond these regular committees, specialized bodies addressed critical governance functions: the Budget Committee's 6 meetings concentrated the intensive work of reviewing departmental spending, capital projects, and tax rate decisions that affect every resident, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings ensured financial accountability and risk management. Throughout these meetings, councillors made decisions spanning infrastructure investments like road repairs and transit expansion, land use planning including residential and commercial developments, public safety resource allocation, social program funding, and the overall municipal budget that balances service delivery with taxpayer affordability.
