London City Council's 2025 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 85 meetings across seven specialized committees addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee and Community and Protective Services Committee led activity with 16 meetings each, handling critical areas such as financial management, human resources, public safety, recreation services, and social programs. Meanwhile, the full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, bylaws, and strategic initiatives that shape London's future.

The committee structure reflects the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings focused on zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies, to the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 sessions dedicated to long-term planning and major policy development. The Budget Committee's six intensive meetings concentrated on the crucial annual budget process, allocating millions in taxpayer dollars across infrastructure projects, public services, and capital investments. Supporting accountability, the Audit Committee's three meetings ensured proper financial oversight and governance practices were maintained throughout the year.
