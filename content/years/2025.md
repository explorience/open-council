London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, holding 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the municipality. The Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees led the workload with 16 meetings each, handling critical functions such as human resources, IT systems, public safety, recreation programs, and social services. Meanwhile, the full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major policy initiatives, bylaws, and strategic directions for the city.

The committee structure reflects the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings focused on zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies, to the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 sessions addressing long-term city planning and policy development. The Budget Committee's six meetings concentrated on the intensive process of reviewing and approving the annual municipal budget, capital projects, and financial planning, while the Audit Committee's three meetings provided essential oversight of financial controls and accountability measures. This meeting frequency demonstrates the ongoing work required to manage infrastructure projects, approve development applications, set tax rates, deliver public services, and respond to community needs throughout the year.
