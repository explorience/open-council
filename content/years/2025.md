London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, conducting 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees were the most active with 16 meetings each, handling critical areas such as financial management, human resources, public safety, recreation services, and social programs. City Council itself held 16 formal meetings to make final decisions on major policy items, while the Planning and Environment Committee met 15 times to review development applications, zoning amendments, environmental initiatives, and urban planning strategies that shape London's growth.

The committee structure reflects the broad scope of municipal governance, with the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee focusing on long-term vision and policy development through 13 meetings, while the Budget Committee's 6 meetings concentrated on the intensive annual budget process that allocates hundreds of millions in taxpayer dollars across city services. The Audit Committee's 3 meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability. Throughout these meetings, council members made decisions on infrastructure investments like road repairs and transit improvements, approved development projects and zoning changes, set property tax rates, funded community programs, addressed public safety concerns, and established policies on everything from climate action to affordable housing.
