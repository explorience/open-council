London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025 with 85 meetings across seven specialized committees, demonstrating the complex and ongoing nature of municipal administration. The Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees led the workload with 16 meetings each, handling critical operational matters such as human resources, financial management, public safety, recreation services, and social programs. Regular City Council meetings, also numbering 16, served as the primary decision-making body where councillors voted on bylaws, policy directions, and major municipal initiatives.

The committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings focused on zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies, to the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 sessions addressing long-term city planning and policy development. The Budget Committee's six concentrated meetings handled the crucial task of setting tax rates, allocating resources across departments, and approving capital expenditures for infrastructure projects like road repairs, transit expansion, and facility upgrades. Meanwhile, the Audit Committee's three meetings provided essential oversight of financial controls and accountability measures, ensuring taxpayer dollars are managed responsibly.
