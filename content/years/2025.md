London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, holding 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services committees led with 16 meetings each, handling critical administrative functions, human resources, financial oversight, public safety, emergency services, and community programs. Meanwhile, the full City Council convened 16 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, bylaws, and strategic initiatives that shape London's future.

The council's work encompassed the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities, from setting annual budgets and tax rates through the Budget Committee's 6 focused sessions, to managing urban growth and development through the Planning and Environment Committee's 15 meetings on zoning applications, environmental assessments, and infrastructure planning. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings addressed long-term city planning, economic development initiatives, and major policy frameworks, while the Audit Committee's 3 meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency. Throughout the year, council members made decisions affecting everything from road repairs and transit systems to housing policies, business licensing, recreation programs, and environmental sustainability measures.
