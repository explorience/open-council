London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2025, conducting 85 meetings across seven specialized committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The workload was distributed fairly evenly among the core committees, with Corporate Services, Community and Protective Services, and full City Council each holding 16 meetings, while Planning and Environment followed closely with 15 meetings. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 13 times to tackle long-term municipal planning and policy development.

Throughout these meetings, councillors made critical decisions spanning municipal budgets, infrastructure investments, zoning amendments, development approvals, public safety initiatives, and community services. The Budget Committee's six focused meetings were dedicated to scrutinizing fiscal planning and resource allocation, while the Audit Committee's three sessions ensured financial accountability and transparency. From approving new residential developments and commercial rezoning applications to funding road repairs, transit improvements, and social programs, the council's work touched every aspect of city life, balancing growth management with fiscal responsibility and community well-being.
