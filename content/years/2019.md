London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2019 with 114 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a major municipal government. The Corporate Services Committee led with 23 meetings, handling crucial administrative functions including budget oversight, human resources policies, and financial management. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with full City Council sessions, each held 20 meetings to address long-term planning initiatives, policy development, and major municipal decisions that shape the city's direction.

The committee structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, from the Planning and Environment Committee's 20 meetings focused on zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives, to the Civic Works Committee's 14 sessions addressing infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings covered social services, public safety, and community programs, while the Audit Committee's 5 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. This comprehensive meeting schedule enables council to make informed decisions on everything from annual budgets and tax rates to individual development permits and community service programs.
