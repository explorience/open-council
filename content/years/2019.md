London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2019 with 114 meetings across seven committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a major municipality. The Corporate Services Committee led with 23 meetings, handling crucial administrative matters including budget oversight, human resources, and financial management. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee and full City Council each convened 20 times to address long-term planning initiatives, policy development, and formal decision-making on major municipal issues.

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the diverse responsibilities of municipal governance, including approving annual budgets and financial expenditures, making zoning decisions that shape urban development, overseeing infrastructure projects like road maintenance and public transit, and ensuring community services meet residents' needs. The Planning and Environment Committee's 20 meetings focused on development applications and environmental sustainability, while the Civic Works Committee's 14 sessions addressed infrastructure maintenance and public works projects. The Community and Protective Services Committee met 12 times to oversee social services, recreation programs, and public safety initiatives, with the Audit Committee conducting 5 meetings to ensure financial accountability and transparency.
