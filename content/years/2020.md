London City Council's 2020 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 112 meetings held across seven specialized committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 24 meetings, reflecting the significant time spent on high-level planning, policy development, and strategic decision-making that guides the city's long-term direction. Regular City Council meetings (21) served as the primary decision-making body where major resolutions, bylaws, and policy directions were formally adopted.

The committee structure reveals the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from Corporate Services handling budgets, human resources, and administrative matters, to Planning and Environment addressing zoning changes, development applications, and environmental initiatives. The Community and Protective Services Committee would have overseen public health, social services, and emergency services, while Civic Works managed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works. The relatively fewer Audit Committee meetings (3) reflects its focused mandate of financial oversight and accountability, typically meeting quarterly to review financial controls and performance.
