The high activity levels of the Planning and Environment Committee (19 meetings) and Corporate Services Committee (19 meetings) reflect the ongoing demands of urban development, zoning applications, environmental stewardship, budget management, and administrative oversight.

These bodies typically make decisions on annual budgets, development applications, bylaw amendments, infrastructure investments, service delivery models, and strategic partnerships. The relatively fewer Audit Committee meetings (3) suggests a focused approach to financial oversight and accountability, while the substantial meeting frequency across other committees indicates 2020 was likely a particularly active year requiring extensive deliberation on municipal priorities, possibly influenced by extraordinary circumstances requiring adaptive governance responses.
