London City Council's 2020 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 112 meetings held across seven specialized committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 24 meetings, reflecting the critical role of long-term planning and policy development in municipal decision-making. This committee, along with the main City Council sessions (21 meetings), would have addressed major strategic initiatives, budget approvals, and city-wide policy directions that guide London's growth and development.

The remaining committees reflect the diverse operational responsibilities of municipal government. The Corporate Services and Planning and Environment Committees each held 19 meetings, handling essential functions like financial management, human resources, zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives. The Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee (13 meetings each) would have focused on public safety, social services, recreation programs, infrastructure maintenance, transportation projects, and public works. The Audit Committee's three meetings ensured financial oversight and accountability. Together, these committees make decisions on everything from annual budgets and tax rates to individual development permits, road repairs, bylaw enforcement, and community programming.
