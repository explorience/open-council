London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2018 with 102 meetings across seven key committees, demonstrating the extensive deliberative work required to manage a major Canadian city. The Corporate Services Committee led with 20 meetings, handling critical administrative functions like budget oversight, human resources policies, and financial management. Meanwhile, City Council itself convened 18 times for formal decision-making on major policy initiatives, bylaw approvals, and strategic direction setting.

The distribution of committee meetings reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government. The Planning and Environment Committee's 18 meetings addressed zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies that shape the city's growth. The Civic Works Committee (15 meetings) focused on infrastructure projects including roads, water systems, and public facilities, while the Community and Protective Services Committee (15 meetings) dealt with social services, public safety, and recreation programs. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 13 times to coordinate long-term planning initiatives, and the Audit Committee's 3 meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability measures.
