London City Council's 2018 work demonstrates the extensive scope and frequency of municipal governance, with 102 meetings held across seven committees throughout the year. The Corporate Services Committee was the most active with 20 meetings, handling critical administrative functions like budget oversight, human resources, and financial management. City Council itself convened 18 times for formal decision-making sessions, while the Planning and Environment Committee also met 18 times to address zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives that shape the city's growth and sustainability.

The distribution of meetings across committees reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government. The Civic Works Committee's 15 meetings focused on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works that keep the city functioning. The Community and Protective Services Committee, also with 15 meetings, addressed social services, public health, emergency services, and community programs. Meanwhile, the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 13 times to guide long-term planning and policy development, while the Audit Committee's three meetings provided essential financial oversight and accountability measures.
