London City Council's 2018 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 102 meetings held across seven committees to address the city's diverse needs. The Corporate Services Committee led with 20 meetings, handling critical administrative functions including budget oversight, human resources, and financial management. City Council itself met 18 times for formal decision-making sessions, while the Planning and Environment Committee also held 18 meetings to review development applications, zoning changes, and environmental initiatives that shape London's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees each played vital roles in municipal operations: Civic Works focused on infrastructure projects like road maintenance, public transit, and utilities through 15 meetings, while Community and Protective Services addressed public safety, social services, and community programs in their 15 sessions. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 13 times to tackle long-term planning and policy development, with the Audit Committee holding 3 meetings to ensure financial accountability. Together, these committees made decisions on everything from annual budgets and tax rates to zoning bylaws, infrastructure investments, development permits, public safety measures, and strategic initiatives that directly impact residents' daily lives.
