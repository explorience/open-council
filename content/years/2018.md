London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2018, conducting 102 meetings across seven key committees throughout the year. The workload was distributed among specialized committees, with Corporate Services leading at 20 meetings, followed closely by regular City Council sessions and Planning and Environment Committee meetings at 18 each. This structure reflects the diverse responsibilities of municipal government, from managing day-to-day operations to long-term strategic planning.

The committee structure demonstrates the breadth of municipal decision-making, encompassing everything from annual budget deliberations and financial oversight to zoning approvals and development applications. Civic Works Committee meetings focused on infrastructure projects like road repairs, water systems, and public facilities, while Community and Protective Services addressed public safety, social programs, and community development initiatives. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 13 meetings indicate significant attention to long-term planning and policy development, while the Audit Committee's three meetings suggest regular financial accountability reviews throughout the year.
