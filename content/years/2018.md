London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2018, convening 102 meetings across seven key committees to address the diverse needs of the municipality. The Corporate Services Committee led with 20 meetings, handling critical administrative functions including budget oversight, human resources policies, and financial management. Regular City Council sessions (18 meetings) served as the primary decision-making forum where councillors voted on bylaws, approved major expenditures, and set strategic direction for the city.

The committee structure reflects the breadth of municipal responsibilities, with Planning and Environment Committee (18 meetings) managing zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives, while Civic Works Committee (15 meetings) oversaw infrastructure projects like road maintenance, public transit, and utility services. The Community and Protective Services Committee (15 meetings) addressed social services, public health, and emergency services, and the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (13 meetings) focused on long-term planning and policy development. With only 3 Audit Committee meetings, this suggests a streamlined approach to financial oversight and accountability review.
