London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the complex, multifaceted nature of municipal governance, with 118 committee meetings addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee led with 29 meetings, handling critical administrative functions including budget oversight, financial planning, human resources, and information technology systems that keep the city running. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee followed closely with 27 meetings, focusing on long-term planning, policy development, and major strategic initiatives that shape London's future direction.

The remaining committees addressed specific operational areas essential to city life. The Planning and Environment Committee's 26 meetings dealt with zoning applications, development approvals, environmental assessments, and urban planning decisions that directly impact neighborhoods and growth patterns. Civic Works Committee's 16 meetings covered infrastructure projects, road maintenance, public works, and capital improvements. Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings addressed public safety, emergency services, recreation programs, and social services. The full City Council convened only 4 times for formal votes and major announcements, while the Audit Committee met 4 times to ensure financial accountability and proper governance practices.
