London City Council's 2015 activities demonstrate the extensive scope and complexity of municipal governance, with 118 committee meetings held throughout the year to address the city's diverse needs. The Corporate Services Committee led with 29 meetings, focusing on financial management, human resources, and administrative operations including budget development and approval processes. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee followed closely with 27 meetings, working on long-term planning initiatives, policy development, and strategic decision-making that shapes the city's future direction.

The remaining committees each played crucial roles in specific areas of city operations: the Planning and Environment Committee (26 meetings) handled zoning changes, development applications, environmental assessments, and urban planning decisions; the Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) addressed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, public works, and transportation planning; and the Community and Protective Services Committee (12 meetings) oversaw public safety, emergency services, social programs, and community development initiatives. With only 4 formal City Council meetings, most detailed work occurred at the committee level, where thorough examination of issues allowed for informed decision-making on matters ranging from multi-million dollar infrastructure investments to neighborhood zoning adjustments that affect residents' daily lives.
