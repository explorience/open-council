London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive scope and complexity of municipal governance, with 118 committee meetings held throughout the year to address the city's diverse needs. The Corporate Services Committee led with 29 meetings, focusing on financial management, human resources, and administrative policies, while the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee held 27 meetings to guide long-term city planning and policy development. The Planning and Environment Committee's 26 meetings addressed crucial zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives that shape London's growth and sustainability.

The remaining committees handled specialized areas critical to city operations: Civic Works (16 meetings) managed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; Community and Protective Services (12 meetings) oversaw social services, public safety, and community programs; while the Audit Committee (4 meetings) ensured financial accountability and transparency. City Council itself convened 4 times to make final decisions on major issues like the annual budget, significant bylaws, and strategic initiatives. This committee structure allows councillors to develop expertise in specific areas while ensuring comprehensive oversight of municipal services, from day-to-day operations to long-term urban planning.
