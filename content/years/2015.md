London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 118 committee and council meetings addressing the diverse needs of city residents. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 29 meetings, focusing on financial management, human resources, and administrative operations including budget deliberations and policy development. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee followed closely with 27 meetings, setting long-term vision and coordinating major city initiatives, while the Planning and Environment Committee's 26 meetings addressed crucial zoning decisions, development applications, environmental assessments, and urban planning matters that shape the city's growth.

The remaining committees tackled equally important municipal responsibilities: Civic Works handled infrastructure projects, road maintenance, transportation planning, and public works with 16 meetings; Community and Protective Services addressed social services, recreation programs, public safety, and emergency services through 12 meetings; while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. The relatively modest number of full City Council meetings (4) reflects an efficient committee system where detailed work occurs at the committee level before final ratification, covering everything from multi-million dollar budget approvals to individual development permits that affect neighborhood character.
