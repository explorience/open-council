London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive behind-the-scenes effort required to govern a major municipality, with 118 committee and council meetings held throughout the year. The highest activity levels were seen in Corporate Services (29 meetings) and Strategic Priorities and Policy (27 meetings), reflecting the significant time devoted to financial management, human resources, strategic planning, and policy development. Planning and Environment Committee's 26 meetings highlight the ongoing work of managing urban growth through zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives, while Civic Works Committee's 16 meetings focused on critical infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works.

The Community and Protective Services Committee's 12 meetings addressed essential services including public health, social services, fire protection, and community programs that directly impact residents' daily lives. With only 4 formal City Council meetings, this structure shows how much of the detailed work happens at the committee level, where councillors can thoroughly examine budgets, review development proposals, debate policy changes, and make recommendations before items reach full council for final approval. This committee system allows for specialized focus on complex municipal issues ranging from multi-million dollar infrastructure investments to neighborhood zoning amendments.
