London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 118 committee and council meetings addressing the city's diverse needs. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 29 meetings, focusing on budget oversight, financial planning, human resources, and administrative policies that keep the city running. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee held 27 meetings to tackle long-term planning initiatives, economic development strategies, and major policy frameworks that guide the city's future direction.

The remaining committees addressed critical municipal functions through regular meetings: Planning and Environment (26 meetings) handled zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental policies; Civic Works (16 meetings) managed infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works; and Community and Protective Services (12 meetings) oversaw public safety, social services, and community programs. With only 4 formal City Council meetings, most detailed work occurred at the committee level, where members could thoroughly examine complex issues like annual budgets, land use planning, infrastructure investments, and service delivery before bringing recommendations to full council for final approval.
