London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive scope of municipal governance, with 118 committee meetings addressing the diverse needs of the city. The Corporate Services Committee led activity with 29 meetings, handling critical administrative functions like budget oversight, financial planning, human resources, and technology infrastructure. Close behind, the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee met 27 times to guide long-term city planning, policy development, and strategic initiatives that shape London's future direction.

The remaining committees each focused on specialized areas essential to city operations. The Planning and Environment Committee's 26 meetings addressed zoning applications, development approvals, environmental protection, and urban planning decisions that directly impact residents' daily lives. Civic Works handled infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works through 16 meetings, while Community and Protective Services met 12 times to oversee social programs, emergency services, and public safety initiatives. The formal City Council convened only 4 times for major decisions and ceremonial functions, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency in municipal operations.
