London City Council's 2015 work demonstrates the extensive scope and frequency of municipal governance, with 118 total committee meetings throughout the year. The Corporate Services Committee was the busiest with 29 meetings, handling essential administrative functions like budgets, financial planning, human resources, and technology infrastructure. Close behind were the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (27 meetings) focusing on long-term city planning and policy development, and the Planning and Environment Committee (26 meetings) addressing zoning applications, development approvals, environmental assessments, and urban planning initiatives.

The distribution of meetings reflects typical municipal priorities, with infrastructure and community services requiring significant attention through the Civic Works Committee (16 meetings) for road maintenance, utilities, and public works projects, and the Community and Protective Services Committee (12 meetings) overseeing social services, public health, emergency services, and community programs. The relatively fewer City Council meetings (4) and Audit Committee meetings (4) indicate these bodies focus on formal decision-making, policy ratification, and financial oversight rather than detailed operational discussions, which are handled at the committee level before recommendations reach full council.
