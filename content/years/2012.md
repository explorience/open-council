The Civic Works Committee's 18 meetings likely focused on infrastructure decisions including road construction and maintenance, public transit, water and sewer systems, and other essential municipal services that keep the city functioning.

The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 16 meetings, combined with 10 full City Council sessions, indicate substantial work on budget deliberations, policy development, and major strategic decisions affecting London's future direction. Meanwhile, the varying frequency of other committees—from the Audit Committee's quarterly meetings to the minimal activity in Corporate Services and Community and Protective Services—suggests either lighter workloads in these areas or the consolidation of certain functions.
