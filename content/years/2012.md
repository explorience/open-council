The Civic Works Committee's 18 meetings likely focused on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, public works, and construction initiatives, while the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 16 meetings would have addressed long-term planning, policy development, and major municipal initiatives.

A typical year of council work involves making critical decisions that affect residents' daily lives and the city's future. This includes approving annual budgets that determine tax rates and service levels, reviewing zoning applications and development proposals that shape neighborhoods, overseeing infrastructure investments in roads, water systems, and public facilities, and setting policies for public safety, community services, and economic development. The distribution of meetings across committees shows how municipal work is specialized - from the technical aspects of planning and engineering to the oversight functions of audit committees and the strategic vision-setting role of policy committees.
