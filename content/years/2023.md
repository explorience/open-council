London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2023, holding 114 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the community. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 24 meetings, reflecting the council's focus on long-term planning and policy development, while core operational committees like Corporate Services (19 meetings) and full City Council sessions (18 meetings) handled essential municipal business. Planning and Environment Committee also met 18 times, addressing crucial zoning decisions, development applications, and environmental initiatives that shape the city's growth and sustainability.

Throughout these meetings, council members made critical decisions spanning municipal budgets, infrastructure investments, zoning bylaws, development approvals, public safety initiatives, and community services. Committees like Civic Works focused on roads, utilities, and public works projects, while Community and Protective Services addressed social programs, housing, and emergency services. The relatively few Audit Committee meetings (3) suggest routine financial oversight, while the full council sessions served as the final decision-making body for major policy directions, budget approvals, and strategic initiatives that impact London residents' daily lives.
