London City Council's 2023 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 114 meetings held across eight specialized committees. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee led with 24 meetings, reflecting the significant time dedicated to long-term planning, policy development, and strategic decision-making that guides the city's direction. Corporate Services (19 meetings) and regular City Council sessions (18 meetings) rounded out the most active bodies, handling essential functions like budget deliberations, financial oversight, human resources, and formal voting on major municipal matters.

The committee structure reveals the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from Planning and Environment Committee's 18 meetings addressing zoning changes, development applications, and environmental initiatives, to Civic Works' 16 meetings focusing on infrastructure projects, road maintenance, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee's 15 meetings covered social services, public safety, and community programs. Notably, the Audit Committee's lighter schedule of 3 meetings reflects its focused mandate of financial oversight and accountability, while the single Community Protective Services Committee meeting suggests either a specialized mandate or potential administrative reorganization during the year.
