London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024 with 112 meetings across eight committees, demonstrating the comprehensive scope of municipal decision-making. The full City Council convened 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled the detailed work of municipal governance. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services, and Planning and Environment committees each met 17 times, reflecting the heavy focus on long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development decisions that shape the city's growth and operations.

Throughout these meetings, council members addressed the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities that directly impact residents' daily lives. This included deliberating on annual budget allocations through 11 dedicated Budget Committee meetings, reviewing zoning applications and environmental assessments, approving infrastructure projects like road repairs and transit improvements, and establishing policies for community services such as recreation programs, public safety initiatives, and social services. The Audit Committee's four meetings ensured financial accountability and transparency in city operations, while committees like Civic Works and Community and Protective Services tackled everything from snow removal contracts to emergency response protocols.
