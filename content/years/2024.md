London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major municipal matters, while specialized committees handled detailed work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant time dedicated to long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development matters. These committees typically address critical municipal functions including urban planning and zoning applications, infrastructure development and maintenance, and corporate policy development.

Throughout the year, council committees focused on core municipal responsibilities that directly impact residents' daily lives. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings were dedicated to financial planning, tax rate setting, and resource allocation across city departments. The Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee each met 14 times to oversee essential services like public safety, social programs, road maintenance, and utility services. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. Together, these committees made hundreds of decisions ranging from routine administrative matters to major policy changes, capital project approvals, development permits, service delivery improvements, and strategic initiatives that shape London's future growth and quality of life.
