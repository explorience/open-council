London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the community. The full City Council convened 18 times throughout the year to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled detailed review and preparation of items within their respective areas of expertise. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant focus on long-term planning, municipal operations, and development issues that shape the city's future.

The committee structure demonstrates the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from the Budget Committee's 11 meetings dedicated to financial planning and resource allocation, to the Community and Protective Services Committee's 14 sessions addressing public safety, social services, and recreation programs. Infrastructure development and maintenance were prioritized through the Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings, while land use planning, environmental protection, and zoning decisions occupied much of the Planning and Environment Committee's time. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential oversight of financial controls and accountability, ensuring transparent use of public resources across all municipal operations.
