London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major municipal matters, while specialized committees handled detailed policy work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant workload in areas such as long-term city planning, administrative oversight, and development approvals including zoning changes and environmental assessments.

The committee structure demonstrates the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from the Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee (14 meetings each) addressing social programs, public safety, and infrastructure projects like road maintenance and utilities, to the Budget Committee's 11 meetings focused on financial planning and resource allocation. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential oversight of financial controls and accountability. Through this comprehensive meeting schedule, council members made critical decisions affecting residents' daily lives, including setting tax rates, approving development applications, establishing service levels, and prioritizing capital investments in roads, parks, and public facilities.
