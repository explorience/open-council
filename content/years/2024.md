London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the municipality. The City Council itself met 18 times for formal decision-making, while specialized committees handled detailed policy work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee were particularly busy with 17 meetings each, reflecting the ongoing demands of long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development management that define municipal governance.

Throughout these meetings, council addressed the fundamental responsibilities of local government: establishing annual budgets through 11 dedicated Budget Committee sessions, reviewing zoning applications and environmental policies, overseeing infrastructure projects and public works, and ensuring community services and public safety measures met residents' needs. The Civic Works and Community and Protective Services committees each held 14 meetings to manage everything from road maintenance and transit systems to emergency services and social programs. Meanwhile, the Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential financial oversight, ensuring taxpayer dollars were managed responsibly across all municipal operations.
