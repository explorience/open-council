London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the municipality. The council's work was distributed across specialized committees, with the main City Council holding 18 meetings alongside key policy bodies like the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (17 meetings) and operational committees covering Corporate Services, Planning and Environment (both 17 meetings each). Community and Protective Services and Civic Works committees each held 14 meetings, while the Budget Committee convened 11 times and the Audit Committee met quarterly with 4 meetings.

This meeting structure reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, where councils make critical decisions affecting residents' daily lives. The Budget Committee's work centers on allocating taxpayer funds across city services, infrastructure projects, and long-term capital investments. Planning and Environment Committee handles zoning changes, development applications, and environmental initiatives, while Civic Works oversees infrastructure maintenance, transportation projects, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee addresses social services, public safety, and community programs, while Corporate Services manages internal operations, human resources, and technology systems. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee coordinates long-term planning and policy development, ensuring the city's various initiatives align with broader municipal goals.
