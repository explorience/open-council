London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major municipal matters, while specialized committees focused on specific areas of municipal responsibility. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services, Planning and Environment committees each held 17 meetings, indicating substantial work in long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development issues. These committees typically handle critical municipal decisions including annual budget approvals, zoning amendments and development applications, infrastructure projects, and policy development.

The committee structure reflects the broad scope of municipal governance, with Community and Protective Services addressing social programs, public safety, and community services, while Civic Works manages infrastructure maintenance, transportation, and public works projects. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings throughout the year demonstrate the ongoing financial planning required to manage municipal resources, tax rates, and capital investments. Meanwhile, the Audit Committee's four meetings provide essential oversight of financial accountability and risk management. This meeting frequency represents the continuous decision-making required to govern a major Canadian city, balancing fiscal responsibility with community needs across areas such as housing policy, environmental initiatives, public transit, recreation services, and economic development.
