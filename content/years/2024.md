London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major policy issues, while specialized committees focused on their respective areas of expertise. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant ongoing work in long-term planning, municipal operations, and development oversight. These committees typically handle critical municipal functions including budget approval, zoning decisions, infrastructure investments, and policy development.

Throughout the year, council work encompassed the full spectrum of municipal governance, from approving multi-million dollar infrastructure projects and setting tax rates through the Budget Committee's 11 meetings, to reviewing development applications and environmental initiatives through Planning and Environment. The Community and Protective Services Committee addressed public safety, social services, and community programs, while the Civic Works Committee oversaw transportation, utilities, and public works projects. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided financial oversight and accountability. This committee structure allows council to thoroughly examine complex issues before bringing recommendations to the full council for final approval, ensuring comprehensive consideration of matters affecting London residents.
