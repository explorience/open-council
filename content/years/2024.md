London City Council's 2024 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 112 meetings held across eight committees throughout the year. The City Council itself met 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled detailed work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee's 17 meetings focused on long-term planning and policy development, while committees like Planning and Environment and Corporate Services also held 17 meetings each, addressing zoning applications, development approvals, financial management, and administrative matters.

The distribution of meetings reflects typical municipal priorities: infrastructure decisions through the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings), community safety and social services via the Community and Protective Services Committee (14 meetings), and financial oversight through both the Budget Committee (11 meetings) and Audit Committee (4 meetings). These committees collectively handle the bread-and-butter work of local government—approving annual budgets, reviewing development proposals, maintaining roads and utilities, ensuring public safety, and overseeing the delivery of essential services that directly impact residents' daily lives.
