London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, convening 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The council's work was distributed among specialized committees, with City Council itself holding 18 formal sessions while the Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee each met 17 times. This structure allowed for focused deliberation on key municipal responsibilities including budget approval and financial oversight, urban planning and zoning decisions, infrastructure development and maintenance, public safety services, and community programs.

Throughout the year, council members made critical decisions that shape London's future, from approving annual budgets and setting tax rates to reviewing development applications and updating zoning bylaws. The committee structure enabled thorough examination of complex issues—whether it was the Civic Works Committee overseeing road construction and utilities, the Community and Protective Services Committee addressing public safety and social programs, or the Audit Committee ensuring fiscal accountability. The 11 Budget Committee meetings reflect the intensive process of allocating municipal resources, while the robust meeting schedules of operational committees demonstrate the ongoing work required to manage a growing city's infrastructure, services, and regulatory framework.
