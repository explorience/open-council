London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The main City Council held 18 meetings throughout the year, while specialized committees met regularly to focus on specific areas of municipal responsibility. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the ongoing demands of long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development management that are central to municipal governance.

Municipal councils like London's make critical decisions that directly impact residents' daily lives, including setting annual budgets that determine tax rates and service levels, reviewing zoning applications and development proposals that shape the city's growth, and overseeing infrastructure projects such as road maintenance, public transit, and water systems. The committee structure allows for specialized focus on areas like community services, protective services (police and fire), environmental planning, and civic works projects. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings demonstrate the significant time and attention required for financial planning and oversight, while the Audit Committee's four meetings reflect the ongoing need for financial accountability and transparency in municipal operations.
