London City Council's 2024 work demonstrates the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 112 meetings held across eight specialized committees. The City Council itself convened 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled the detailed work of municipal administration. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services, Planning and Environment committees each met 17 times, reflecting the heavy workload around long-term city planning, development applications, zoning decisions, and corporate governance matters. Meanwhile, the Community and Protective Services and Civic Works committees each held 14 meetings to address public safety, social services, and infrastructure maintenance.

The Budget Committee's 11 meetings highlight the significant time dedication required for financial planning, tax setting, and resource allocation across city departments. Throughout the year, council committees would have deliberated on a wide range of municipal matters including property tax rates, development permits and zoning amendments, road construction and transit projects, bylaw enforcement, social housing initiatives, and capital infrastructure investments. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided oversight of financial controls and accountability measures, ensuring taxpayer dollars are properly managed across all city operations.
