London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, holding 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the municipality. The full City Council convened 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees focused on their respective areas of responsibility. The Planning and Environment Committee, along with Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant time dedicated to managing urban development, zoning applications, environmental initiatives, and long-term strategic planning that shapes London's future growth.

The committee structure demonstrates the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from Corporate Services handling administrative and financial matters, to Civic Works overseeing infrastructure projects like road maintenance, water systems, and public works. The Community and Protective Services Committee addressed social programs, public safety, and community well-being, while the Budget Committee's 11 meetings were crucial for establishing spending priorities and tax rates. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential oversight of financial accountability. Through these meetings, council members made decisions on everything from annual budgets and development approvals to bylaw amendments, infrastructure investments, and community service delivery.
