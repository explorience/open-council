London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The main City Council held 18 meetings while specialized committees focused on their respective areas of responsibility. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee each met 17 times, indicating significant activity in long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development matters. Meanwhile, the Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee each convened 14 times to address social services, public safety, and infrastructure needs.

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the fundamental responsibilities of municipal government, including approving annual budgets and financial plans, making zoning and development decisions that shape the city's growth, overseeing infrastructure projects like road maintenance and water systems, and establishing policies for community services such as recreation programs, public health initiatives, and emergency services. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings reflect the intensive process of allocating taxpayer resources, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings ensured proper financial oversight and accountability in municipal operations.
