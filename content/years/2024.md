London City Council's 2024 meeting schedule reflects the comprehensive scope of municipal governance, with 112 meetings held across eight committees and full council sessions. The work was distributed among specialized committees that handle distinct areas of city operations: Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee focused on long-term planning and policy development, while Corporate Services managed internal operations, human resources, and financial oversight. The Planning and Environment Committee addressed zoning applications, development proposals, and environmental initiatives, while Community and Protective Services oversaw public safety, social services, and community programs.

Throughout these 112 meetings, council members made critical decisions affecting residents' daily lives, from approving annual budgets and capital infrastructure projects to reviewing development applications and updating municipal bylaws. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings concentrated the intensive work of allocating taxpayer dollars across city services, while committees like Civic Works handled infrastructure maintenance, transportation planning, and public works projects. This meeting structure ensures thorough deliberation on complex issues ranging from housing policy and economic development to environmental protection and public transit, demonstrating how municipal government touches virtually every aspect of urban life through careful planning, regulation, and service delivery.
