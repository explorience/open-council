London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, holding 112 meetings across eight different committees and the main council chamber. The full City Council convened 18 times throughout the year, while specialized committees handled the bulk of detailed policy work, with Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, Corporate Services Committee, and Planning and Environment Committee each meeting 17 times. This committee structure allows Council to thoroughly examine complex municipal issues before bringing them to the full council for final decisions.

The committee meetings focused on the core responsibilities of municipal government: budget planning and oversight through the dedicated Budget Committee's 11 sessions, urban development and zoning decisions via the Planning and Environment Committee, infrastructure projects and public works through the Civic Works Committee, and community services and public safety matters in the Community and Protective Services Committee. The Corporate Services Committee handled internal municipal operations and human resources, while the Audit Committee provided financial oversight with quarterly meetings. This comprehensive meeting schedule reflects the diverse scope of municipal governance, from approving multi-million dollar infrastructure projects and setting property tax rates to reviewing development applications and establishing community programs.
