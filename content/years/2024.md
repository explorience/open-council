London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, holding 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council convened 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled detailed work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each met 17 times, reflecting the significant focus on long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development decisions that shape the city's growth and character.

Throughout these meetings, council members addressed the fundamental responsibilities of municipal government, including approving annual budgets and financial plans through dedicated Budget Committee sessions, making critical zoning and development decisions that determine how the city evolves, and overseeing infrastructure projects ranging from road maintenance to major capital investments. The Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee meetings focused on essential services like public safety, social programs, and municipal operations, while the Audit Committee ensured financial accountability and transparency in city operations.
