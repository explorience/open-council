London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled detailed work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant attention given to long-term planning, administrative oversight, and development issues. Community services, infrastructure projects, and financial planning rounded out the council's core responsibilities through their respective committees.

Throughout these meetings, council members tackled the fundamental decisions that shape city life: approving annual budgets that determine spending on essential services, reviewing zoning applications that guide urban development, authorizing infrastructure projects like road repairs and facility upgrades, and establishing policies for public safety, transit, and community programs. The Budget Committee's 11 meetings demonstrate the extensive deliberation required for financial planning, while the Planning and Environment Committee's active schedule reflects London's ongoing growth and development pressures. From routine operational matters to major strategic initiatives, this meeting structure ensures thorough democratic oversight of municipal affairs.
