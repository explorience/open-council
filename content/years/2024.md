London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024 with 112 meetings across eight committees, demonstrating the extensive work required to manage a major Canadian municipality. The City Council itself met 18 times throughout the year, while specialized committees each held between 4 and 17 meetings to address their respective portfolios. These committees tackle the full spectrum of municipal responsibilities: the Budget Committee's 11 meetings focused on financial planning and resource allocation; Planning and Environment Committee's 17 sessions dealt with zoning applications, development approvals, and environmental initiatives; while Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings addressed critical infrastructure projects including roads, water systems, and public facilities.

The committee structure reflects the diverse nature of municipal governance, with Corporate Services handling administrative matters and human resources, Community and Protective Services overseeing public safety and social programs, and Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee guiding long-term city planning and policy development. The Audit Committee's four meetings, though fewer in number, provide essential oversight of financial controls and accountability. This comprehensive meeting schedule enables Council to make informed decisions on everything from multi-million dollar infrastructure investments and annual budget approvals to individual development applications and bylaw amendments that directly impact residents' daily lives.
