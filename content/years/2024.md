London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The City Council itself met 18 times for major decision-making, while specialized committees handled specific portfolios with Strategic Priorities and Policy, Corporate Services, and Planning and Environment each holding 17 meetings. These committees tackle the fundamental aspects of municipal governance: setting strategic direction and policy frameworks, managing corporate affairs and financial oversight, and reviewing development applications and environmental initiatives.

The committee structure reflects the broad scope of municipal responsibilities, from the Community and Protective Services Committee and Civic Works Committee (14 meetings each) addressing public safety, recreation, infrastructure maintenance, and public works projects, to the Budget Committee's 11 meetings focused on financial planning and resource allocation. The Audit Committee's four meetings provided essential oversight of financial controls and accountability. Throughout the year, council members would have made decisions on annual budgets, zoning amendments and development approvals, infrastructure investments, bylaw updates, public safety initiatives, and strategic planning for the city's growth and sustainability.
