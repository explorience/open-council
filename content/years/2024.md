London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, holding 112 meetings across eight committees to address the diverse needs of the city. The full City Council met 18 times to make final decisions on major policy matters, while specialized committees handled detailed work in their respective areas. The Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee, along with Corporate Services and Planning and Environment committees, each held 17 meetings, reflecting the significant attention given to long-term city planning, organizational management, and development issues. These committees typically address crucial municipal decisions including annual budget approvals, zoning amendments and development applications, infrastructure investments, and service delivery policies.

Throughout the year, council work encompassed the full spectrum of municipal governance, from reviewing multi-million dollar infrastructure projects and transportation planning in the Civic Works Committee (14 meetings) to addressing community safety, social services, and protective services through the Community and Protective Services Committee (14 meetings). The Budget Committee's 11 meetings were focused on the critical work of allocating taxpayer resources across city departments and services, while the Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided oversight on financial accountability and operational efficiency. This structure ensures comprehensive governance covering everything from road repairs and transit systems to housing policies, economic development initiatives, and environmental protection measures that directly impact residents' daily lives.
