London City Council maintained an active governance schedule in 2024, conducting 112 meetings across eight committees and full council sessions. The 18 City Council meetings served as the primary decision-making body where major policies, bylaws, and strategic initiatives were debated and voted upon. The committee structure reflects the diverse scope of municipal governance, with Strategic Priorities and Policy Committee (17 meetings) focusing on long-term planning and major policy directions, while Corporate Services Committee (17 meetings) handled administrative matters, human resources, and financial management.

The committee work demonstrates the breadth of municipal responsibilities, from Planning and Environment Committee's 17 meetings addressing zoning changes, development applications, and environmental initiatives, to Community and Protective Services Committee's 14 meetings covering public health, social services, and emergency services. Civic Works Committee's 14 meetings focused on infrastructure projects, roads, water systems, and public works, while the Budget Committee's 11 meetings were concentrated on financial planning, tax setting, and resource allocation. The Audit Committee's 4 meetings provided oversight of financial accountability and risk management, ensuring transparency in the city's operations.
